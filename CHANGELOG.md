# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first, written for customers. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) once the first release ships.

## [Unreleased]
### Fixed
- Text and spacing across the app were about a fifth smaller than designed; fields, checkboxes and buttons are now their intended size.
- Emails now name the product: "Confirm your email for Vento Hotels" and "Reset your Vento Hotels password".

### Added
- The tape chart: every room of a hotel by night, with each booking as a bar, rooms out of order hatched, each room's status (Inspected, Clean, Dirty, Out of order) and how many rooms of each type are left. Give a booking a room or move it by dragging its bar onto a room (rooms free every night light up, taken ones say why), with Undo; or click it for **Room move**, which lists ready rooms first and other room types with the price difference. Moving to a better room type shows the new price, or **Keep** the booked price as an upgrade recorded as a discount, approved by a manager over your limit. Drag a bar's end to extend or shorten the stay with the price shown first, click a free night to book that room, and use the keyboard throughout (arrows, Enter, M, E, T, N). **Mark clean** and **Mark dirty** from a room's number, even offline (they sync when you're back). GMs, housekeeping supervisors and maintenance can **put a room out of order** for dates with a reason: Vento refuses it while a booking is in the room or when it would leave too few rooms, and rooms left drop everywhere at once; every room taken off sale is in the owner's daily report. On phones the chart is a one-night list by room type. Reservations now show their room, with Room move on the booking. A room holds one booking a night, guaranteed. The demo group has rooms assigned for the coming week, room statuses and rooms out of order.
- Change, cancel and mark no-shows: on a reservation, **Modify** (or press M) changes the dates, room type or guests with the new price shown first (nights already agreed keep their price, new nights are at today's rate, and you see which room types are free); **Cancel** shows the policy, the penalty and the nights released, and asks for a reason; **More › Mark no-show** releases tonight's rooms and records the no-show penalty. **Waive the penalty** with a reason: managers waive it themselves, front desk asks a manager, who approves with their PIN at the desk or from their inbox, and the waiver applies the moment they do. Every waiver is in the owner's daily report with both names. Cancelled and no-show bookings show who ended them and why, a Penalty card, and **Copy cancellation** for the guest.
- Manager approval for discounts over your limit: on Review & book, **Ask for approval** lets a manager with a higher limit approve it at your desk with their PIN (on the hotel's registered front desk PC), or from their own phone through their inbox while you wait; you book once they say yes. The bell in the top bar shows requests waiting for you, and the new **Approvals** page lists Waiting, Asked by you and Done for the hotel you're looking at (or all of them). Every approved discount records who gave it and who approved it, with the reason. An approval is for that one booking at that price, used once, and lasts 30 minutes; nobody can approve their own. Wrong PINs count with the lock screen's. Approvals need a connection.
- Take bookings: Reservations › **New reservation** (or press N) books a room in four steps. See how many rooms are left on every night for each room type, pick a plan with its price per night (excl. or incl. taxes) and total incl. taxes, find a returning guest by name, phone or email or add a new one, and book. The summary shows every night, the service charge and taxes, the deposit due and the cancellation terms, and for a foreign guest the total in their currency at the day's CBN rate. Give a discount within your limit with a reason (recorded for the owner), or book through a min stay with a reason. Two people can't sell the same last room: the second is told it was just sold, and nothing is saved. Each booking gets a number like VI-2611-0483 (a 2–4 letter prefix per hotel, set in Settings › Hotels until the first booking) and a page with the nights and taxes, the guest, the deposit and cancellation terms, what happened, and **Copy confirmation** to paste into WhatsApp with the hotel's bank account and reference. The list finds any booking by name, phone, email or number, shows Upcoming, Booked today and All, and filters by status, room type, how they booked, arrival dates and deposits due; with All hotels it shows every hotel. New bookings need a connection; the list stays readable offline. Removing rooms on a night that's fully booked now says how many rooms short it would be, and a hotel with upcoming bookings can't be archived. Rate plans show what guests pay per night incl. taxes. The demo group has about 450 bookings.
- Taxes, payments and FX rates in Settings. **Taxes and charges** sets up a hotel's VAT, state consumption tax and service charge with one click ("Set up for Lagos"); every rate is marked To verify until someone records its legal source with Mark verified. Rates never change in place: schedule a change (or a stop) from a future business date with a source and reason, cancel it before it starts, and see "How a bill adds up" for any amount, excl. or incl. taxes, on any date. **Payments** switches each payment method on or off (with Undo), lets cash take US dollars, pounds or euros, and keeps each company's bank accounts (masked in lists) and each hotel's POS terminals; one account is marked as where guests transfer, and changing it on a live hotel emails the owners at once. **FX rates** takes the day's CBN rates in seconds, checks a figure far from the last one, keeps replaced rates in the history, and shows each currency's last 30 days. Changes on live hotels ask for a reason and show in the owner's daily report. Home's checklist gains "Set up taxes and payments". The demo group has all of it, with illustrative rates.
- Set your rates: Rates & channels › Rate plans starts with "Your first rate", one weekday price per room type (and a Friday and Saturday price if it differs), with Bed & breakfast at +₦18,000 in the same click. Add plans with their own prices or that follow another (Non-refundable at Best available − 10%, with a live preview of each room type's price), and set what's included (room only, breakfast with its food share), the cancellation policy, the deposit, min stay, an extra adult charge and who can book it (anyone, or corporate accounts only). Duplicate, reorder, archive and restore plans. The new Rate calendar shows 14 or 30 nights from the business date for every plan and room type: click a price to change one night, or use Change dates for a range with days of the week, a season name, min stay, closed to arrival and closed, each with Undo; plans that follow another change with it. Every price says it's excl. taxes and service charge. Once a hotel is live, lowering tonight's price asks for a reason that reaches the owner's daily report. Front desk staff can read rates to quote callers. Rooms shows each type's base rate, Home's setup checklist gains "Set rates", and rates open read-only offline.
- The hotel switch now scopes every page. Pick Kora Abuja and Members and roles shows the people who work there (with owners and group-wide people marked "Whole group") and its invites, Devices shows its devices, and Companies the company that owns it; pick All hotels and you see everyone and everything, each with its hotel. Every page says above its title which hotel it shows, or "Whole group" where it's the same everywhere (Permissions and limits), and so does the browser tab. New invites and devices start on the hotel you're looking at. Links Vento makes to one hotel (from Home, for instance) open on that hotel. On phones the switch has the whole top bar, with the business date in the account menu.
- Hotels and companies in Settings: complete each hotel's address, logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 10 MB), check-in and check-out times and owning company, and each company's RC number, TIN and VAT registration. Add a hotel in under two minutes; it joins the hotel switch at once, the top bar moves to it, and Next steps take you to its rooms, team and devices. Archive a hotel that has closed (it shows exactly what happens first, and asks why), and restore it later with its rooms and people's access. Companies can be added, edited, archived and restored, and a company that still owns hotels says which to move first. With "All hotels" chosen you see every hotel and company; with one hotel chosen, just that hotel and the company that owns it. GMs can change their hotel's check-in and check-out times. Home's setup checklist gains "Complete hotel and company details", and hotel logos show on Home.
- Set up your rooms: Rates & channels › Rooms holds each hotel's room types and rooms. "Add rooms by floor" builds a whole hotel in minutes: name your room types, add lines like "Deluxe King · floors 4 to 6 · numbers 01 to 14", check the live preview (click any room to change its type or leave it out), and create every room in one go. Edit a room's number, floor, type and features, mark it accessible or link connecting rooms; change the type of many rooms at once (a page, or every room your filters show) or remove them, with Undo and an Archived list to restore from. Each room has an audit trail of every change, who made it and why. New rooms start dirty, with one click to mark them all inspected. Once a hotel is live, removing a room or changing its type asks for a reason that's recorded with your name. Home's setup checklist gains "Set up rooms", and Home shows each hotel's room count. The page works on phones, and offline shows your saved rooms read-only.
- Emails are retried if sending fails, instead of being lost: confirmation and invite emails go through a background queue that tries again for several minutes. Behind the scenes, Vento now keeps structured logs with a reference for every request and reports unexpected errors to the team, without any personal data.
- Keeps working through power and internet cuts: once you've used Vento on a device, it opens without a connection and shows what you last saw (your group, hotels and Home), with a clear "You're offline" banner saying when it was saved. It notices Wi-Fi that has no internet, and clears by itself when the connection is back. When a new version is ready, Vento asks before reloading so it never interrupts your work. Signing out, or locking a shared device, removes everything saved on it.
- Home speaks to your job and your hotel. A navy welcome banner greets you by name with the date, your group and hotel. Below it, figures for your job: front desk sees arrivals, departures and in-house guests with New reservation and Walk-in at hand; housekeeping sees dirty rooms and inspections; a room attendant their rooms; waiters and cashiers their tables and shift; finance, maintenance, stores, sales, revenue and HR their own figures. Each figure fills in as its module goes live, and until then the tile says which one brings it. Then your hotel (business date, check-in and check-out, people and shared devices), "Needs attention": what's worth fixing now, each with one button to fix it, and "Your workspace": a card for every module you use, with what it's for. Owners and group admins also get a "Finish setting up" checklist that highlights the next step and ticks itself off as you go.
- All hotels: owners, group admins and group finance can choose "All hotels" in the hotel switch. Home then shows the whole group: figures for your role across the group (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and revenue for owners; revenue, city ledger and cash variances for finance, each filling in as its module goes live) and every hotel in a row; click one to see that hotel's day, and "All hotels" in the banner to come back. Your choice is remembered on each browser.
- Demo data for trying Vento and for sales demos: Kora Hotels Group with four hotels and their 382 rooms in four room types, eleven people in every kind of role (each with a sign-in and a staff PIN), shared devices, a pending invite and a permission change, loaded with one command.
- Light, dark or follow your device: a three-way Appearance switch in the account menu, remembered on each browser. On phones the account menu opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- Two-step sign-in: turn it on from the account menu with an authenticator app, save 10 backup codes, and sign in with a 6-digit code after your password. "Don't ask again on this computer for 30 days" for your own computer. Owners and group admins see it as a step in Home's setup checklist until they turn it on, and Members and roles shows who has it.
- A calmer, more polished look: page titles with status badges, full-width cards for anything not built yet, more generous margins, and the hotel shown as a quiet pill at the top left.
- The full menu: every module, grouped as Front office, Revenue, Operations and Back office, with Settings at the foot. Each person sees only the modules their role uses, and only modules switched on for their group. Modules still being built say what they'll do and which stage brings them.
- Preview as a role: owners and group admins can see the app as a front desk agent, waiter, GM or any other role would, with a clear banner and one click back.
- Shared devices: register a restaurant tablet, front desk PC or housekeeping phone to a hotel in Settings › Devices, then staff tap their name and enter a 4-digit PIN to sign in. Devices sign people out when left idle (90 seconds on restaurant tablets, 5 minutes elsewhere), and there's a Lock button for the next person. Everyone sets their own PIN from the account menu; managers can reset one. Five wrong PINs lock it for 15 minutes. A lost device can be revoked, signing everyone out of it. On a shared device you only see that hotel, and settings need your email and password.
- A wider, easier-to-read sidebar (280px).
- Bring your team in: owners and group admins invite people by email with a role and the hotels they'll see, resend or cancel invites, change someone's role or hotels, and remove them. Invited people join from the email in one step (name and password, no second confirmation email). Owners see every role's permissions and limits side by side, or one role at a time on a phone, and change a rule or limit with a reason. Every change is in the audit log. Settings appears in the sidebar for people who can use it.
- Sign in, and set up a hotel group: create your account, confirm your email, then add your group, company and first hotel. Opens in light mode; works on phones. Passwords need at least 8 characters with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a special character, with a checklist that ticks each one as you type. Forms check each field as you move on and clear the message once it's fixed, suggest a fix for mistyped email providers ("Did you mean …@gmail.com?"), warn when Caps Lock is on and show when passwords match. Passwords can be shown while typing, sign-up asks you to confirm yours, and a forgotten password can be reset by email (the link lasts 30 minutes and signs you out everywhere else).
- A better front door and shell, everywhere: every tab is titled by its screen; a "Check your inbox" screen with Resend (once a minute), "Wrong email? Start again" and "Back to sign in"; signed-in people skip sign-in and sign-up; the hotel setup form checks each name as you go, picks your state from all 36 and the FCT, and offers "Not you?" to sign out; a page that doesn't exist offers the way back; an account menu with your name, email and Sign out; the business date and connection status fit on phones; touch targets are at least 44px; a clear focus ring for keyboard users; "Can't reach Vento right now" when the connection drops, keeping what you typed. Every page is checked for accessibility in light and dark mode on desktop and phone, and the app's download size has a budget it can't exceed.
- Every change to your group's setup and permissions is recorded in an audit log that nobody can edit.
