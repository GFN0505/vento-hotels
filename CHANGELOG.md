# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first, written for customers. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) once the first release ships.

## [Unreleased]
### Fixed
- Text and spacing across the app were about a fifth smaller than designed; fields, checkboxes and buttons are now their intended size.
- Emails now name the product: "Confirm your email for Vento Hotels" and "Reset your Vento Hotels password".

### Added
- Emails are retried if sending fails, instead of being lost: confirmation and invite emails go through a background queue that tries again for several minutes. Behind the scenes, Vento now keeps structured logs with a reference for every request and reports unexpected errors to the team, without any personal data.
- Keeps working through power and internet cuts: once you've used Vento on a device, it opens without a connection and shows what you last saw (your group, hotels and Home), with a clear "You're offline" banner saying when it was saved. It notices Wi-Fi that has no internet, and clears by itself when the connection is back. When a new version is ready, Vento asks before reloading so it never interrupts your work. Signing out, or locking a shared device, removes everything saved on it.
- Home speaks to your job and your hotel. A navy welcome banner greets you by name with the date, your group and hotel. Below it, figures for your job: front desk sees arrivals, departures and in-house guests with New reservation and Walk-in at hand; housekeeping sees dirty rooms and inspections; a room attendant their rooms; waiters and cashiers their tables and shift; finance, maintenance, stores, sales, revenue and HR their own figures. Each figure fills in as its module goes live, and until then the tile says which one brings it. Then your hotel (business date, check-in and check-out, people and shared devices), "Needs attention": what's worth fixing now, each with one button to fix it, and "Your workspace": a card for every module you use, with what it's for. Owners and group admins also get a "Finish setting up" checklist that highlights the next step and ticks itself off as you go.
- All hotels: owners, group admins and group finance can choose "All hotels" in the hotel switch. Home then shows the whole group: figures for your role across the group (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and revenue for owners; revenue, city ledger and cash variances for finance, each filling in as its module goes live) and every hotel in a row; click one to see that hotel's day, and "All hotels" in the banner to come back. Your choice is remembered on each browser.
- Demo data for trying Vento and for sales demos: Kora Hotels Group with four hotels, ten people in every kind of role (each with a sign-in and a staff PIN), shared devices, a pending invite and a permission change, loaded with one command.
- Light, dark or follow your device: a three-way Appearance switch in the account menu, remembered on each browser. On phones the account menu opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- Two-step sign-in: turn it on from the account menu with an authenticator app, save 10 backup codes, and sign in with a 6-digit code after your password. "Don't ask again on this computer for 30 days" for your own computer. Owners and group admins see it as a step in Home's setup checklist until they turn it on, and Members and roles shows who has it.
- A calmer, more polished look: page titles with status badges, full-width cards for anything not built yet, more generous margins, and the hotel shown as a quiet pill at the top left.
- The full menu: every module, grouped as Front office, Revenue, Operations and Back office, with Settings at the foot. Each person sees only the modules their role uses, and only modules switched on for their group. Modules still being built say what they'll do and which stage brings them.
- Preview as a role: owners and group admins can see the app as a front desk agent, waiter, GM or any other role would, with a clear banner and one click back.
- Shared devices: register a restaurant tablet, front desk PC or housekeeping phone to a hotel in Settings › Devices, then staff tap their name and enter a 4-digit PIN to sign in. Devices sign people out when left idle (90 seconds on restaurant tablets, 5 minutes elsewhere), and there's a Lock button for the next person. Everyone sets their own PIN from the account menu; managers can reset one. Five wrong PINs lock it for 15 minutes. A lost device can be revoked, signing everyone out of it. On a shared device you only see that hotel, and settings need your email and password.
- A wider, easier-to-read sidebar (280px).
- Bring your team in: owners and group admins invite people by email with a role and the hotels they'll see, resend or cancel invites, change someone's role or hotels, and remove them. Invited people join from the email in one step (name and password, no second confirmation email). Owners see every role's permissions and limits side by side, or one role at a time on a phone, and change a rule or limit with a reason. Every change is in the audit log. Settings appears in the sidebar for people who can use it.
- Sign in, and set up a hotel group: create your account, confirm your email, then add your group, company and first hotel. Opens in light mode; works on phones. Passwords need at least 8 characters with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a special character, with a checklist that ticks each one as you type. Forms check each field as you move on and clear the message once it's fixed, suggest a fix for mistyped email providers ("Did you mean …@gmail.com?"), warn when Caps Lock is on and show when passwords match. Passwords can be shown while typing, sign-up asks you to confirm yours, and a forgotten password can be reset by email (the link lasts 30 minutes and signs you out everywhere else).
- A better front door and shell, everywhere: every tab is titled by its screen; a "Check your inbox" screen with Resend (once a minute), "Wrong email? Start again" and "Back to sign in"; signed-in people skip sign-in and sign-up; the hotel setup form checks each name as you go, picks your state from all 36 and the FCT, and offers "Not you?" to sign out; a page that doesn't exist offers the way back; an account menu with your name, email and Sign out; the business date and connection status fit on phones; touch targets are at least 44px; a clear focus ring for keyboard users; "Can't reach Vento right now" when the connection drops, keeping what you typed. Every page is checked for accessibility in light and dark mode on desktop and phone, and the app's download size has a budget it can't exceed.
- Every change to your group's setup and permissions is recorded in an audit log that nobody can edit.
