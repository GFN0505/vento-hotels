## Problem
At 2 p.m. the desk has a queue, and nothing in Vento says who is arriving, who is leaving, or who is already in the house. A guest stands at the counter while the agent finds the booking, writes the passport details on a paper card, shouts to housekeeping to ask whether 502 is ready, and hands over a key; the system never learns the guest is in. A walk-in is worse: the room is sold, the money taken and the card filled in, and the booking may never be typed at all, which is exactly how room nights disappear. When immigration or the police ask for the register, it's a pile of paper cards with half the foreign national fields blank. And when a guest in 503 has to move because the AC failed, there's no way to move someone who is already in house without losing which room they slept in last night.

## Persona and job
- **Front desk agent** (Chiamaka Eze, FD-VI-01, PIN session, keyboard first): opens the Front desk lists at the start of the shift and works them all day; checks in 20–40 arrivals a day, most pre-assigned, some unassigned, a few foreign nationals; does 2–5 walk-ins a day, often in the evening; moves in-house guests when something breaks. Busiest at 12:00–15:00, with a queue.
- **Night auditor** (Ibrahim Musa, same PC, night shift): checks in late arrivals after midnight on the same business date; reads the in-house list.
- **GM** (Tunde Bello): approves a walk-in discount with his PIN; reveals a passport number when an immigration officer asks; reverses a check-in made by mistake; reads the lists from his phone.
- **Owner** (Mrs Adaeze Okafor): sees the house at a glance (Tonight strip), and the next morning every reversed check-in and every discount with names (brick 16); trusts that the register is complete if the authorities ask.

## Success looks like
- **Walk-in in under 60 seconds** for a new Nigerian guest, one night, no approval: from **W** to "checked in to 401", keyboard only (timed e2e; the time at the desk is recorded on `stay.checked_in` as `durationSeconds`, and the pilot's median is under 60 s). The 60 s includes the registration and the card print request; it excludes the payment (brick 10) and any approver's wait.
- **Check-in of a pre-assigned returning guest in 2 clicks** (Check in → Check in to 404), under 30 seconds; a first-time foreign national in under 2 minutes trained, under 5 for a new agent.
- **Zero checked-in stays without a room**, and **zero rooms with two in-house stays on a night** (database constraint per night plus the service check under the room lock; race tests).
- **Zero full ID, passport or visa numbers** in any API response other than the reveal procedure, in logs, audit events, PDFs, the service-worker cache or the saved-data store (contract test scanning responses and stored rows; seed test).
- **Every foreign national checked in has the fields required by the rule set in force** for the hotel's state (server check), so the guest register (brick 15) is complete without anyone chasing paper.
- **Every reversed check-in** is in the owner's daily report with both names and the reason.
- **Offline:** an assigned arrival on the cached list checks in with the internet off, syncs once (idempotency test), and shows "Will sync" until it does.
- The Front desk lists show the house in **under 1 second** on the desk PC for a 120-room hotel.

## Acceptance criteria

### Main path
**What this brick delivers**
1. The **Front desk** page: Arrivals, Departures and In house for the business date, with search, pills, keyboard and print; the phone version as cards.
2. **Check-in** of a `confirmed` stay arriving on the business date: registration (with the foreign national section), the room (confirm the assigned one, or pick one ready-first), and the A4 **registration card**. Undo within 8 seconds; **Reverse check-in** later the same business date by a GM.
3. **Walk-in**: book and check in in one transaction, under 60 seconds.
4. **In-house** stays: moved through brick 7's service and dialog from the business date on; departure extended or shortened through brick 6's change service.
5. **Registration records** stored per guest per stay, ID and visa numbers encrypted at rest and masked everywhere, revealed only with a permission and a reason.
6. The **tape chart** and its Tonight strip show in-house stays; the free-room rule counts `checked_in` everywhere `confirmed` holds a room.
7. **Offline** check-in of a cached, assigned arrival through the outbox.
8. Demo seed: in-house guests across Kora Victoria Island and Kora Ikeja GRA, named arrivals to try every rule below.

**Check-in (#6)**
- **Who:** `stay.check_in` (new row). **What:** a `confirmed` stay of a `confirmed` reservation, arrival = business date. Earlier in the day than the hotel's check-in time is allowed and shown ("Early check-in · before 14:00"; the fee comes with brick 10). After midnight before night audit, the business date is still 25 Nov, so a 01:30 arrival checks in on 25 Nov.
- **Not offered** (button disabled with the reason): arrival later ("Arrives 26 Nov 2026. Check-in opens on the arrival day."), arrival passed ("Arrival was 24 Nov 2026. Mark it as a no-show."), cancelled, no-show, already checked in ("Checked in by Ibrahim Musa at 01:12.").
- **The sheet** (stepped, design #6): title "Check in · Daniel Mensah", subtitle "VI-2611-0482 · 502 · 25–28 Nov 2026 · 3 nights". Steps: **Verify ID**, **Registration**, **Room** (Deposit and Key come later). Back (⌘←), Continue (↵), Close (Esc, with the leave guard when anything was typed).
- **Registration step:**
  - **Guest:** the stay's guest. **Someone else is staying** swaps the stay's guest for another (guest search or new, brick 4's fields), keeping the booker on the reservation; audited.
  - **Fields** come from the **registration rule set** in force for the hotel's state on the business date (configuration, below): required ones shown, optional ones under **More details**. The Stage 1 seed (all To verify, Open question 1): lead guest: title, first and last name, nationality, phone or email (one required, from the profile), home address (line, city, state or region, country; Nigerian states from `NIGERIAN_STATES`), **ID type** (Nigerian: NIN, International passport, Driver's licence, Voter's card; foreign nationals: Passport, or ECOWAS national ID card for ECOWAS nationals) and **ID number**; optional date of birth, purpose of visit (Business, Leisure, Other), vehicle plate.
  - **Foreign national registration** (any nationality but NG; opens by itself, and when the nationality is changed to a foreign one): passport number (the ID above), issuing country (defaults to the nationality), passport expiry, **occupation**, **visa or permit** (Visa on arrival, Business visa, Tourist visa, Transit visa, Residence permit (CERPAC), Other with text, and **Not required · ECOWAS citizen**, preset for ECOWAS nationals and offered only to them), visa or permit number and valid until (hidden for Not required), date of entry to Nigeria, port of entry (Lagos MMIA, Abuja NAIA, Port Harcourt PHIA, Kano MAKIA, Enugu, Seme border, Other), arriving from, **next destination**.
  - **Other guests in the room** (**Add guest**): each further adult by name and nationality; a foreign adult gets the same foreign national section and their own registration record (Open question 1). Children aren't registered.
  - **Warnings, never blocks:** passport or visa expiring before departure ("Visa ends 26 Nov 2026, before departure on 28 Nov."); passport expired ("Passport expired 3 Mar 2026. Check the guest's travel document."). The hotel records; it doesn't enforce immigration law.
  - **Returning guest:** if the guest has an earlier registration in the group, its fields prefill and the ID line reads "Same NIN as last stay · ••••••6612 · Change". Kept, the server copies the encrypted number; the full number never goes to the device. Changed, the new number replaces it for this stay only.
  - **Booking said Nigerian, passport says otherwise:** the agent changes the nationality; the guest profile is corrected (audited, as brick 4's corrections).
  - Field rules: dates as date pickers; ID numbers trimmed, upper-cased, 4–20 letters, digits or hyphens; NIN exactly 11 digits ("A NIN has 11 digits."); errors on leaving the field.
- **Verify ID step (ID photo or scan; the owner, 28 Nov 2026: "Nigerian hotels request for a photo or document of the ID, if it's physical it can be scanned"):**
  - The agent checks the physical ID against the guest and captures it: **Take photo** opens the device camera (phone camera or the desk webcam; `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="environment">`), **Upload scan** takes an image or PDF from a scanner. The photo shows as a local preview ("Check the photo is sharp and every corner shows") with **Retake**; nothing leaves the device until the check-in is saved.
  - Required for the **lead guest** by default, through the same registration rule set (field `id_document`, To verify; a state or hotel can turn it off or make it apply to foreign nationals only). Other guests' IDs aren't photographed in Stage 1.
  - On the device: images are downscaled to at most 1600px on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG (quality 0.85), which drops EXIF and other metadata (location, camera); PDFs are sent as they are. Over 5 MB after that: "That file is over 5 MB. Take a photo instead, or scan at a lower resolution."
  - On the server: the type is checked from the file's own bytes (JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF; anything else: "That isn't a photo or a PDF. Take a photo of the ID, or upload a scan."), JPEG metadata segments are stripped again, 5 MB cap, stored in the **private bucket** only, under a key made of ids (`guest-ids/{group}/{hotel}/{document}.jpg`, no names or numbers), never in the public bucket, never in logs.
  - **Returning guest:** an earlier ID document of the same guest in the group whose ID expiry hasn't passed is reused ("ID photo on file from 14 Oct 2026 · NIN ••••••6612 · Change"); **Change** takes a new one.
  - **Without a photo:** a GM or owner (`guest.id_skip`) can **Skip for now** with a reason ("Camera not working"); then the stay shows **ID photo to add** on the In house list and the reservation, and **Add ID photo** there attaches one later (same rules, audited). The front desk can't skip a required photo ("Only a GM or the owner can skip the ID photo. Ask your GM.").
  - **Viewing a saved ID image** needs the same permission as Reveal (`guest.id_view`) and a reason; it opens through a signed link that works for 60 seconds, is never cached by the service worker, and writes `guest.id_document_viewed`. Others see "ID photo on file" with no View button.
  - **Kept** with the guest's registration and archived with it; never hard-deleted while history exists. The retention period is an open question for the lawyer (`docs/DOMAIN.md`).
- **Room step:**
  - The assigned room is shown first with its readiness: "502 · Executive King · Clean · Chiamaka Eze · 11:10". **Change room** opens brick 7's list in place.
  - No room yet (or **Change room**): `stays.roomOptions` with **ready rooms first** (Inspected, then Clean), then not ready (Dirty; "Due out" rooms whose checked-in guest leaves today), each with features ("City view", "Connects to 402"). **The booked room type only** while it has a room free for every night; other types, as an upgrade through brick 7's move-to-another-type rules (Charge {type} rate by default, Keep with a discount and approval), only when it's full (owner's rule, 2026-11-27). Up to 8 per type, **Show all 23**.
  - **A room that isn't ready can be chosen** (decision for this brick): inline amber "403 is dirty (Grace Akpan · 08:50). The guest can register now and get the key when it's clean."; due out: "402: Kwame Asante is due out today and hasn't left yet." The primary reads **Check in to 403 · room not ready**. Refusing would push agents to mark rooms clean that aren't, or back to paper, and the room status is the truth housekeeping relies on. Ready rooms always come first, so the default is right, and the stay shows **Waiting for room** until the room is marked clean or inspected.
  - Rooms out of order tonight, taken, archived or too small aren't listed; a stale pick is refused under the lock (brick 7's errors).
- **Check in:** one transaction (lock order below): the stay `confirmed → checked_in` with who, when, device, business date; the reservation `checked_in` once all its stays are (one in Stage 1 screens); the room set on every night from the business date (a new assignment if none or changed, through brick 7's assign rules: `stay.room_assigned` or `stay.room_moved` with `source: 'check_in'`); registration records written; audit events. Nothing about folios or keys (brick 10, key cards later).
- **Print registration card** is a checkbox under the primary, on by default, remembered per device. On success the PDF opens in the print dialog. Also from the reservation detail (**Print registration card**) and the in-house row menu; a later print is a reprint.
- **Undo** (8 seconds, toast): back to `confirmed`, the room kept as assigned, registration records kept (they're true); `stay.check_in_undone`. After that, **Reverse check-in** (GM, below).
- **Two agents, one guest:** the second gets "Chiamaka Eze checked in Daniel Mensah at 11:35." (`ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`); the same idempotency key returns the first result.
- **A check-in left open when the PC locks** (PIN idle sign-out) is discarded with what was typed (personal data isn't left for the next person); the arrival stays on the list.

**Walk-in (#6, 60 seconds)**
- **Who:** `stay.check_in` and `reservation.manage`. Online only (below).
- **Page** "Walk-in" (hotel page, W from the lists, Home's quick action, ⌘K "Walk-in"). Restrictions show as brick 4's warnings with book-through and reason. **Change rate** is brick 4's discount: within the limit at once; over it, brick 5's prompt (kind `stay_discount`), **Approve 15% and check in**.

- **Last room taken meanwhile:** "Standard Queen was just sold for 25 Nov. Pick another type." (`SOLD_OUT`), or "302 was just taken. 303 is ready." with the next room preselected (`ROOM_TAKEN`). Nothing is saved.
- **Undo** (8 seconds) cancels the walk-in reservation (brick 6's cancel, reason Duplicate, no penalty) and releases the nights; after that: Reverse check-in, then cancel.
- No Day use (same-day arrival and departure) in Stage 1.

**In-house stays**
- **The room is kept per night.** Nights before the business date (closed by night audit, or simply past) keep the room they were in; a move or any change touches nights from the business date on. The tape chart draws an in-house stay that moved as two segments (earlier nights muted in the old room, a small move marker, the rest in the new room); the stay's history lists both.
- **Room move (brick 7's service and dialog, `room.move`):** a `checked_in` stay with at least one night left (departure after the business date; "Emeka Kalu leaves today. There's no night left to move." otherwise). Subtitle "503 Executive King · in house since 23 Nov · 2 nights left (25–27 Nov)". **Reason always required** (pills Guest request, Maintenance, Upgrade, Noise, Other with text). The new room must be free on every remaining night; ready rooms first; dirty allowed with the warning. Same type: no inventory change, Undo (8 seconds, moves back if the old room is still free and puts its status back). Another type: brick 7's rules (only when the booked type is full; Charge {type} rate or Keep with the discount and `modify_discount` approval), **priced on the remaining nights only**; past nights keep their price and room. A paid upgrade the guest asks for while the booked type has rooms is a **Modify** room type change, which for an in-house stay asks for the new room in the same dialog.
- **The old room becomes dirty** through the room status service, in the move's transaction (`room.status_changed`, `source: 'room_move'`), whatever it was: a guest has used it.
- **Drag on the tape chart:** dragging an in-house bar moves the part from the business date on and opens the move popover with the required reason; the past part doesn't move. Its right end extends or shortens.
- **Extend or shorten (brick 6, `reservation.manage`):** Modify (or the drag) changes the **departure** only; arrival, plan and past nights are fixed. New nights at today's plan price; the room must be free on them ("Room 502 is booked from 28 Nov (Kalu E.). Move the stay to a free room first."), and an in-house stay is never unassigned. Shortening keeps at least the business date's night (departure ≥ business date + 1; leaving today is check-out, brick 10) and follows brick 6's late-change penalty and waiver.
- In-house stays can't be cancelled or marked no-show: the reservation detail doesn't offer Cancel or Mark no-show on them, and the server refuses both with brick 6's not-allowed answer. Leaving early is check-out (brick 10).

**Reverse check-in (GM)**

### Offline behaviour
- **Readable offline:** the Front desk lists last loaded, under the banner "Offline since 17:32. Check-ins save on this computer and sync later. Room readiness is as of 17:32." and "Last updated 17:32"; the tape chart as brick 7.
- **Works offline (decision 0009's allow-list): check-in of an arrival on the cached list that already has a room.** The sheet works from the cached row (guest name, nationality, room and readiness; no earlier registration to reuse, since nothing personal is cached: all required fields are typed). **Check in to 502** applies at once on the device (the row moves to Checked in today with "Will sync") and queues `stays.checkIn` in the outbox with an idempotency key, the stay version, the room, the registration, the device time and business date. Change room is disabled offline ("Picking a room needs a connection."), as is Someone else is staying.
- **The queued registration's ID numbers and the ID photo** are encrypted on the device (WebCrypto AES-GCM, a non-extractable key per device in IndexedDB), shown masked in the sync queue, and deleted from the device when the command is answered (applied or needs attention). On sync the photo is uploaded first (idempotent by its id), then the check-in names it.
- **Sync:** in order per person (brick 7's outbox). The server re-checks everything under its locks. Outcomes: applied; duplicate (same key: the first result); **needs attention**, never silently dropped: room taken or out of order meanwhile ("Daniel Mensah's check-in to 502 couldn't sync: 502 went out of order at 11:40. Open the check-in to pick another room." → **Open check-in**, with the typed registration kept on the device until done or discarded), already checked in by someone else ("Ibrahim Musa checked Daniel Mensah in at 11:36. Your details weren't saved." → **Apply details**, which sends them as a registration correction), stay changed or cancelled meanwhile, or the business date moved on before it synced ("The business date moved to 26 Nov before this check-in synced. Ask the night auditor.").
- **Blocked offline** (live availability or full numbers needed): viewing a saved ID image, walk-in ("Walk-ins need a connection to check live availability."), picking or changing a room, in-house moves and extends ("Moving rooms needs a connection."), reverse check-in, reveal, printing the card ("Print available when you're back online."), registration corrections. Disabled with the reason; nothing queued.
- **What stays on the device:** today's and tomorrow's lists for the device's hotel (names, booking numbers, rooms, nationality, readiness; no phone, email, address or ID numbers), kept through PIN locks and person switches on the front desk PC, and cleared on full sign-out, device revoke and when the business date moves on (decision 0029). ID photos are never kept, except encrypted in a queued check-in.
- A request sent just before the connection dropped is retried with the same key ("Checking whether Daniel Mensah was checked in…"); never twice.

### Business date

### Availability locking
- **Check-in of an assigned arrival, room unchanged:** hotel FOR SHARE (2) → reservation FOR UPDATE (2b) → the room FOR UPDATE (3); re-check it's free every night (another stay meanwhile, out of order). No inventory rows (the nights were already booked).
- **Check-in with a new or changed room (same type):** brick 7's assign path, 2 → 2b → 3 (old and new rooms in id order). **Another type** (only when the booked type is full): brick 6's modify with the room, 1 → 2 → 2b → 3 → 4 → 5, inside the check-in transaction.
- **Walk-in:** brick 4's booking with a room: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7. Two agents taking the last Standard Queen: one wins, the other `SOLD_OUT` with nothing saved (race test, 200 runs).
- **In-house move:** as brick 7 (same type 2 → 2b → 3; another type 1 → 2 → 2b → 3 → 4 → 5), the old room's status written under its lock (3), inventory only for the remaining nights.
- **Reverse check-in:** 2 → 2b → 3 (the room, for its status).
- **Constraint:** one stay per room per night for `confirmed` and `checked_in` stays (Data model). Race tests: two check-ins into the same free room for different arrivals (one wins); a check-in races an out-of-order on its room (one wins); an in-house move races an assignment into the target room; an offline check-in synced after the room was given away returns `conflict`.

### Empty, error and edge cases
- **No arrivals today:** "No arrivals left today. All 38 guests are checked in. Tomorrow has 31 arrivals." → **Tomorrow** (opens the Reservations list for the next date) and **Walk-in** (design). None at all: "No arrivals at Kora Abuja today." → **Walk-in**. No departures: "No departures today." In house empty: "Nobody is in house at Kora Abuja." Departures and In house have no action.
- **Loading:** the design's row skeletons; **error:** "Couldn't load arrivals. The list didn't load; nothing you did was lost. Check-ins already made are saved." → **Try again** (R).
- **Assigned room went out of order or was taken:** the row shows rose "503 is out of order" / "502 was given to Kalu E." with **Pick another room** (opens check-in at the Room step).
- **Party bigger than the room** (booking changed): "506 sleeps 2 adults and 1 child." (`ROOM_TOO_SMALL`); pick another room.
- **Registration incomplete:** Check in moves focus to the first problem ("Enter the guest's passport number", "Enter the next destination"); the server refuses `REGISTRATION_INCOMPLETE` with the field names if the client missed one.
- **Booked type full and no other type fits:** "No Deluxe King room is free for all of 25–27 Nov, and no other room type fits 2 adults and 1 child. Move a booking on the tape chart first."
- **Booking changed meanwhile:** brick 6's `RESERVATION_CHANGED` ("Sade Bakare changed this booking at 11:08. Check the new details.").
- **Archived hotel, plan or room:** read-only hotel; an archived plan can't sell a walk-in ("Bed & breakfast was archived."); an archived assigned room: "Room 412 was removed. Pick another room."
- **Undo too late:** "Undo has expired. A GM can reverse the check-in from the reservation." (`UNDO_EXPIRED`).
- **Idempotency key reused with different details:** "This check-in was already saved." with the result.
- **Long names, many companions, 30-night walk-in:** names truncate with a tooltip in lists, wrap on the card; the card stays one page with up to 4 companions (a second page after that).
- **Departure passed, still in house** (only until brick 10 and in dev): Departures shows "Due out 24 Nov"; the room counts occupied only on nights the stay covers.
- **Imported in-house guests (brick 17):** come in as `checked_in` stays with their room and a registration marked "Registered before Vento" (fields as available, required ones not enforced for imports).
