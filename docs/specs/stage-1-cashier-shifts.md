## Problem
Every naira that crosses the front desk passes through a drawer, and today nobody can say whose drawer it was or what should be in it. Agents start the day with a float someone handed them, take cash, POS and transfers all shift, and at hand-over they count the drawer with the expected figure in front of them, so a shortage gets "found" by matching the number, and an overage quietly leaves in a pocket. When the till is short, the morning and afternoon agents blame each other and the GM can't tell when it happened. The cashier needs to start a shift with a float she has counted and accepted, finish it with a count she makes without knowing what the system expects, and have any difference recorded with her reason, a manager's name when it's large, and the owner seeing it the next morning. Payments can't be built until there is an open shift for them to land on.

- **Front desk agent** (Chiamaka Eze, morning 07:00–15:00, FD-VI-01 PIN session or her own sign-in, drawer Desk 1): opens a shift at the start of every shift, counts and closes at the end. Twice a day, every day; the close is done with guests waiting and a colleague ready to take over, so it must take under 3 minutes.
- **Night front desk agent** (Ibrahim Musa, 23:00–07:00, Desk 2): the same, with a shift that runs across the night audit.

- **GM** (Tunde Bello): approves a difference over the agent's limit with his PIN at the desk or from his inbox; covers an afternoon shift himself sometimes; reads every shift of his hotel.
- **Finance** (Kunle Ade): sets up the hotel's cash drawers once; reads every shift; later reconciles safe bags (Stage 5).
- **Owner** (Mrs Adaeze Okafor): next morning, every difference with the cashier's name, amount, reason and approver (brick 16).

## Success looks like
- **Open a shift** with a hand-over that matches in **1 click after typing the note counts** (Open shift), and a walk-through of the count by note in under 60 seconds for a ₦50,000 float.
- **Close a balanced shift** in **4 clicks after typing** (Count and close → Submit count → confirm → Close shift), the whole close including counting under 3 minutes (as `waiter-dinner-service.md` targets).
- **Zero** expected totals or differences returned by any procedure, to anyone, for a shift whose count hasn't been submitted (contract test: the open shift's response type has no expected fields; API test over every cash procedure).
- **Zero** changes to a submitted count: no update path in the service, and a database trigger refuses `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on count lines (test).
- **Zero** shifts closed with a difference and no reason; **zero** differences above the cashier's limit closed without an approver; every non-zero difference at a live hotel lands in the owner's daily report with names (tests).
- **At most one** unclosed shift per drawer and per person, even when two people press Open at the same moment (database constraints; 200-run race test).
- All amounts are `numeric(14,2)` with their currency; foreign cash keeps the rate, source and date used (tests with USD and GBP).

## Acceptance criteria

### Main path
**What this brick delivers**
1. **Cash drawers** per hotel: a name and the POS terminals at it; every hotel has at least one ("Desk 1", created for every existing and new hotel). Set up on Settings › Payments.
2. **Open a shift** on a free drawer with a float counted by note, compared with the float the drawer's last shift left (the **hand-over**); a difference needs a reason, and above the limit an approval.
3. **Count blind**: cash by note, foreign cash per currency, each POS terminal's merchant slips, bank transfers; submitted once, then locked.
4. **Close**: expected vs counted per line, a reason for any difference, an approval above the limit, the float kept in the drawer and the rest dropped to the safe with a bag number.
5. **Shifts list and shift detail** for the hotel (own shifts for everyone who runs a shift, all shifts for managers and finance).
6. The **expected totals seam for brick 10**: `expectedFor(shift)` returns lines by method, currency and terminal from the opening float plus (from brick 10) the shift's payments and cash refunds; snapshotted at submit.
7. Approval kind `cash_variance`; demo seed; owner's review steps.

**Drawers**
- A **cash drawer** belongs to a hotel: **Name** (unique among the hotel's active drawers, up to 30 characters, e.g. "Desk 1", "Desk 2"), **POS terminals at this drawer** (any of the hotel's active terminals; a terminal may sit at more than one drawer). Archived, never deleted.
- A migration creates **Desk 1** at every hotel, with the hotel's active POS terminals if it has exactly one; creating a hotel (brick 0) creates Desk 1 in the same transaction.
- Settings › Payments gains a **Cash drawers** card (`finance.setup`): Drawer · POS terminals · Now ("Shift 1 open · Chiamaka Eze", or "Free · ₦50,000.00 float"); **Add drawer**, **Edit**, **Archive drawer**, **Restore**. Archive is refused while the drawer has a shift not closed, and for the hotel's last active drawer. An archived drawer's left float isn't carried to any other drawer (the archive dialog says "₦20,000.00 float was left in Desk 2. Take it to the safe."). Archiving a POS terminal (brick 3) removes it from drawers.

**Open a shift** (`cashier_shift.operate`)
- **Preconditions:** the person has no shift that isn't closed, anywhere in the group (one at a time); the drawer has no shift that isn't closed (one at a time; money is still in it until close).
- **Drawer:** pills of the hotel's active drawers when there are several; a busy one shows "Desk 1 · Chiamaka Eze's shift" and can't be picked. Default: the drawer last opened in this browser, else the first free one. One drawer: no picker.
- **Float counted by note:** rows ₦1,000, ₦500, ₦200, ₦100, ₦50 (whole numbers of notes, 0–99,999, empty = 0) and **Smaller notes and coins** (an amount, ₦0.00–₦99,999.00). Row totals and **Float counted** update as she types (decimal helpers). The note list is a constant in `@vento/shared` (`NGN_NOTES`), not a setting. A float of ₦0.00 is allowed ("Open shift with no float").
- **Hand-over (shown, not blind):** if the drawer's last shift closed with a float kept, the aside shows who left it, which shift, when, and the amount, and compares live: "matches" (emerald), "₦2,000.00 short" or "₦2,000.00 over". First shift on a drawer (or the last one left ₦0.00): "First shift on Desk 2. Count the float you were given." and nothing to compare. Why not blind: the incoming cashier's own close is measured against the float she accepts, so she has every reason to count it properly; blindness matters at close, where the cashier knows what she took.
- **Difference at hand-over:** any difference needs **Reason for the difference** (required, 5–200 characters; suggestions "Topped up the float from the safe", "Counted wrong at the last close"). Measured in naira against the opener's `cashier_shift.variance` limit; above it, **Ask for approval · ₦8,000.00** (kind `cash_variance`, stage `handover`); the approval is used in the open transaction.
- **Open** records the shift: hotel, drawer, number (per hotel per business date, from 1, in opening order), person, device, business date and time opened, float counted (by note), the hand-over (previous shift, float handed over, difference, reason, approver). Status **open**.
- The request carries an idempotency key and a client-made id (UUID v7); a double click or retry returns the same shift. (Brick 10 can then put opening on the offline allow-list without changing the contract.)
- **Hand-over changed meanwhile** (the drawer's previous shift closed or another opened between load and Open): refused, the aside refreshes: "Desk 1 was just closed by Tunde Bello at 22:50. Check the hand-over."

**My shift** (the person's own shift at the selected hotel)
- **Open:** "Cashier shift 1 · 25 Nov 2026", badge **Open**, "Chiamaka Eze · Desk 1 · opened 06:55", **Float ₦50,000.00**, "Takings stay hidden until you count, so your count is your own." Primary **Count and close**. No takings, totals or "expected" anywhere, to anyone, until the count is submitted.
- **Counted, not closed** (she submitted then left the page, or her PIN session locked): the page opens on step 2, ready to close.
- Her shift at another hotel (switch changed): "Your shift 1 is open at Kora Victoria Island (Desk 1)." with **Go to Kora Victoria Island** (`?hotel=`); she can't open another until it's closed.

**Count blind (step 1 of 2)** (only the shift's own person, status `open`)
- **Lines to count**, in this order, built by the server for the shift (no amounts):
  1. **Cash ₦ in the drawer, including float**: by note as at opening, total **Counted**.
  2. **Cash** in each foreign currency the hotel's cash takes (USD, GBP, EUR from brick 3): one amount per currency ("Cash US$", hint "Total in dollars"), 0.00–999,999.99, empty = 0.
  3. **POS terminal** for each terminal at the drawer, and (from brick 10) any other terminal a payment on the shift used: "POS terminal · Moniepoint · Desk 1", hint "Total of your merchant slips this shift". **Required** (0 allowed): "Enter the slip total, or 0 if the terminal took nothing this shift."
  4. **Bank transfers** (when bank transfer is on at the hotel), NGN: hint "Total of transfers you confirmed this shift". **Required** (0 allowed). Foreign-currency transfer lines appear only when a shift has one (brick 10).
  - Methods that are off aren't shown. City ledger isn't money in the drawer and isn't counted. Online card (Paystack, Stage 4) will be counted automatically, nothing to enter.
- Required lines left empty: focus moves to the first ("Enter the slip total, or 0 …"). Whole notes only ("Enter a whole number of notes."); over 99,999 notes: "That's more than 99,999 notes. Check the count."
- **Submit count** asks once: "Submit your count? Cash ₦50,000.00 · US$0.00 · £0.00 · POS ₦0.00 · Transfers ₦0.00. You can't change it after you submit." → **Submit count** / **Keep counting**. (A confirm, not Undo: this is the one moment the count must be deliberate.)
- **On submit, in one transaction:** the shift row is locked; status must be `open`; `expectedFor(shift)` is computed and stored on each line beside the count (the snapshot: later payments can't change it, and from brick 10 no payment can attach to a shift that isn't open); each line's difference = counted − expected; foreign lines store the FX rate used (the hotel's latest rate dated on or before the business date, from brick 3), its source and rate date, and the NGN equivalents (rounded half-up to the kobo once); the shift's **total difference** = the sum of the absolute differences of every line in naira (a short in cash and an over on POS don't cancel out). Status **counted**. The response is the first time expected figures leave the server.
- **No foreign rate:** if a foreign line has a count above 0 or an expected above 0 and the group has never entered a rate for that currency: refused, "There's no US$ rate yet. Ask your GM to enter today's FX rates, then submit." Nothing is saved. A rate older than the business date is used and named ("at ₦1,620/$, CBN, 23 Nov 2026").
- **A submitted count can't be changed** by anyone (Open question 5). A second submit: "This count was submitted at 14:46 and can't be changed." (same idempotency key: the stored result).
- The typed count lives only on the screen: if the PIN session locks or the page closes before submit, it's gone and she counts again (no partial count is stored, so nobody can submit someone else's figures).

**Close (step 2 of 2)** (only the shift's own person, status `counted`)
- **Takings by method** table: Method · Expected · Counted · Difference · Source. "Cash ₦ (incl. ₦50,000.00 float)" · Drawer count; "Cash US$" · "Drawer count · ≈ ₦64,800.00 at ₦1,620/$, CBN, 25 Nov 2026"; "POS · Moniepoint · Desk 1" · Your merchant slips; "Bank transfers" · Your transfer log. Differences: "₦0.00" muted, "−₦2,500.00" / "+₦12,500.00" in rose with "short" / "over" in words (never colour alone). The count by note is shown below, read-only.
- **Close shift** panel: badge **Balanced** (emerald), **Short** or **Over** (amber); the difference line ("Cash ₦2,500.00 short", or "Total difference ₦5,000.00 · cash ₦2,500.00 short, POS ₦2,500.00 over"); the limit line "Within your ₦5,000.00 limit" or "Over your ₦5,000.00 limit. A manager can approve it here."
- **Reason for the difference:** required when the total difference isn't ₦0.00 (5–200 characters; suggestions "Change given in error", "Paid out from the drawer", "Counting error at opening"). "Recorded with your name."
- **Float stays in drawer:** naira, default the shift's opening float, from ₦0.00 to the naira cash counted ("The float can't be more than the ₦459,500.00 counted."). **To the safe** = naira cash counted − float kept, plus all foreign cash counted (foreign cash is never part of a float: change is given in naira, brick 3). **Safe bag number** required when anything goes to the safe (up to 20 letters, digits, hyphens; "Enter the safe bag number.").
- **Within the limit:** **Close shift · drop ₦409,500.00 to safe** (or **Close shift** when nothing goes to the safe). **Over the limit:** **Ask for approval · ₦12,500.00** (kind `cash_variance`, stage `close`), then **Close shift** once approved, or the approver's **Approve and close shift** on the same screen.
- **On close, in one transaction:** shift row locked; status must be `counted`; reason present when the total difference isn't zero; the total difference re-checked against the person's limit now; an approval, if given, locked and checked (brick 5's rules: same hotel, kind, requester, binding, not used, not expired, approver still eligible) and marked used; float kept, safe drop (NGN and foreign amounts), bag number, close time and business date stored. Status **closed**. The drawer is free, holding the float kept.
- Toast "Shift 1 closed · ₦409,500.00 to the safe in bag S-2211" / "Shift 1 closed · balanced". The page then offers **Open a new shift**.

**Shifts list and detail** (hotel page)
- **Cash** page tabs **My shift** and **Shifts**. **Shifts** lists the selected hotel's shifts, newest first, server-paginated `{ items, page, pageSize, total }`, 50 a page; segments **All**, **Open** (open and counted), **With a difference**; business date range (default the last 7 business dates). Columns: Shift ("Shift 3 · 24 Nov"), Drawer, Cashier, Opened–closed (times, with the date when it crossed midnight), Float, Difference ("Balanced", "₦2,500.00 short", "₦12,500.00 over", "Counted at close" while open), Status (Open blue, Counted amber "Count in, not closed", Closed slate), Approved by.

An open shift's detail shows the float and "Counted blind at close", nothing else.
- No export, print or report here: the printable cashier / shift report is brick 15.

**Ready for brick 10 (contract, not built here)**
- `payments.shiftId` will be NOT NULL: every payment and cash refund is on the taker's shift with status `open` at that hotel; without one, Take payment says "Open a shift to take payments" with **Open cashier shift**. In this brick it returns the opening float on the cash ₦ line and 0.00 elsewhere.
- A POS terminal may sit at more than one drawer (Moniepoint · Desk 1 at Desk 1 and Desk 2): brick 10 attributes each POS payment to the taker's open shift, not to the terminal, so a shared terminal never mixes two shifts' slips.
- Nothing in brick 10 may show a running takings total for an open shift (no "My takings today"), or the count stops being blind.

### Organization isolation

The approval is bound by a hash the server computes: for `handover`, the hotel, drawer, previous shift, float counted by note, difference and reason; for `close`, the shift, its submitted count (lines and total difference) and reason. Changing the reason after asking cancels the request ("Request to Tunde cancelled") and asks again.
- Running a shift, asking and approving work in a PIN session. Setting up drawers is refused in a PIN session (brick 3's rule for `finance.setup`).
- Behind the `front_desk` switch (shifts, drawers card, the `cash_variance` kind).
- **Proposed matrix rows** (`docs/DOMAIN.md` and `packages/shared/src/permissions.ts`, Front office group): rename "Open / close cashier shift" to **"Open, count and close own cashier shift"** (key `cashier_shift.operate`, same roles); new **"Close a cashier shift with a difference"** (`cashier_shift.variance`, limit `amount_ngn`; seed: front_desk 5,000, cashier 5,000, fnb_manager 20,000, general_manager 100,000, finance and owner / group admin no limit).

### Audit events
All with `recordAudit` in the action's transaction: actor, device, property, business date, before/after, reason, approver (`approverMemberId`).
- `cashier_shift.opened`: shift id and number, drawer, float counted (by note and total, NGN), hand-over (previous shift id, cashier, float handed over, difference, reason), approval id and method; `exception: 'cash_variance'`, `stage: 'handover'` and `liveProperty` when the hand-over difference isn't ₦0.00.
- `cashier_shift.count_submitted`: shift, every line (kind, currency, terminal, counted, expected, difference, FX rate, source and date, NGN equivalents), total difference.
- `cashier_shift.closed`: shift, total difference and lines' differences, reason, float kept, safe drop (NGN, foreign), bag number, approval id, method and approver; `exception: 'cash_variance'`, `stage: 'close'` and `liveProperty` when the total difference isn't ₦0.00.
- `approval.requested`, `.granted`, `.declined`, `.cancelled`, `.expired` (brick 5's events) with kind `cash_variance`; the subject names the hotel, drawer, shift, cashier, lines and amounts, reason and limit, never guest data.
- `cash_drawer.created`, `.changed` (name, terminals before and after), `.archived`, `.restored` (with reason).
- Wrong PINs at the prompt follow brick 5 (nothing per try; `pin.locked` on lockout).

### Leakage exceptions
- **Cash variance** (existing exception type, now defined): every shift closed with a total difference other than ₦0.00, and every hand-over with a difference, at a live hotel (`liveProperty: true`; before go-live recorded, not reported). On the business date of the close (or the opening for a hand-over). Owner's daily report lines (brick 16):
  - "Cash variance · Shift 3 · 24 Nov, Desk 2 · Cash ₦12,500.00 over · Ibrahim Musa · Reason: late check-out fee taken in cash on the old system during the parallel run · Approved by Tunde Bello"
  - "Cash variance · Shift 1 · 24 Nov, Desk 1 · Cash ₦2,500.00 short · Chiamaka Eze · Reason: paid a guest's taxi from the drawer"
  - "Float ₦2,500.00 over at hand-over · Desk 1 · left by Chiamaka Eze ₦47,500.00, counted by Tunde Bello ₦50,000.00 · Reason: topped up the float from the safe"
- Every difference, not only those over the limit: the limit decides who signs, not whether the owner sees it (as discounts). Balanced shifts, the float kept and safe drops are audited facts, not exceptions. A shift left open or counted but not closed is brick 13's night audit check.

### Offline behaviour
- **Readable offline:** the person's own shift card (number, drawer, float, opened time, status) and the Shifts list as last loaded, under the banner. Nothing with expected figures is cached for a shift that isn't counted (there is nothing to cache: the server never sent it).
- **Blocked offline in this brick:** opening a shift (the one-per-drawer and hand-over checks are only true on the server), submitting a count (the expected snapshot is computed on the server), closing, asking or giving approvals, drawer setup. Buttons are disabled with "Opening a shift needs a connection." / "Submitting a count needs a connection. Keep counting; your figures stay on this screen." / "Closing a shift needs a connection." Typed counts stay on the screen while offline; nothing is queued.
- Banner: "Offline since 17:32. Showing your shift as of 17:32. Opening, counting and closing a shift need a connection."
- A request sent just before the connection dropped is retried with the same key when it returns, if the screen is still open ("Checking whether your count was submitted…"); never twice.
- **Brick 10** decides, with offline cash payments, whether opening joins the allow-list (the contract is ready: client-made id and idempotency key; conflicts on sync would be "Desk 1 already has Ibrahim Musa's shift open" → needs attention). Close stays online-only (`offline-sync` skill).

### Business date
- A shift records the hotel's business date when it opened (its number is per hotel per business date), when its count was submitted and when it closed. Night audit can roll the date while a shift is open (the night shift); the shift keeps its opening date, and its close and any exception land on the business date of the close.
- The FX rate for foreign cash is the hotel's latest rate dated on or before the business date at submit.
- Approvals expire when the business date moves on (decision 0027): a counted shift whose approval expired asks again.
- What night audit does with shifts not closed is brick 13.

### Foreign currency
- Foreign cash is counted in its own currency (USD, GBP, EUR as the hotel's cash takes), with 2 decimals; expected, counted and difference are stored in that currency, with the FX rate (4 decimals), source (`cbn`; `market` arrives with brick 10) and rate date, and NGN equivalents rounded half-up once (`toAmount`). The limit and the total difference use the NGN equivalents. Display: "$40.00" with "≈ ₦64,800.00 at ₦1,620/$, CBN, 25 Nov 2026" in muted text (brick 3's helper).

- **Large amounts:** ₦999,999,999,999.99 fits `numeric(14,2)`; tables keep alignment with long amounts; 99,999 notes cap per row.

- **Idempotency key reused with a different request:** `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` "This was already saved." with the result.
- **Two tabs:** the second's Submit gets the first's result or `COUNT_ALREADY_SUBMITTED`; both then show step 2.

## Open questions for the owner
**Answered 2026-11-28:** the owner chose the recommendation for questions 1–4. Question 5 wasn't put to the owner; the recommendation (no reopen in Stage 1) stands and is flagged in the pull request.

1. **Is a cashier shift per person, or per cash drawer?** Recommended: **per drawer and per person: a shift is one person on one named drawer ("Desk 1"); a drawer has one shift open at a time, and a person has one shift open at a time across the group. The float left in a drawer is handed over to the next shift on it, which counts it.** Why: money sits in a physical drawer, not a login. Tying the shift to the drawer is what makes a hand-over checkable ("Ibrahim left ₦50,000, Chiamaka counted ₦48,000"), and one person per drawer means a difference always has exactly one name on it. Shared drawers (two agents in one till) are where most front desk shortages can't be traced.
2. **Which takings are counted blind at close?** Recommended: **as the design: cash in naira counted by note, foreign cash counted per currency, each POS terminal's merchant slips added up and entered, and the total of bank transfers she confirmed entered, all without seeing the expected figures; online card (Paystack, from Stage 4) is counted automatically. City ledger isn't counted (no money changes hands).** One change to the design: the POS line asks for "the total of your merchant slips this shift", not "the end-of-day slip", because a terminal's end-of-day report covers the whole day across shifts, while the merchant copies in her drawer are exactly her shift; the terminal's day total is matched in brick 11.
3. **What difference needs a manager's sign-off?** Recommended: **any difference needs a reason and goes in the owner's daily report; a total difference above ₦5,000.00 per shift (front desk and cashiers; F&B manager ₦20,000.00, GM ₦100,000.00, finance and owner any) needs a manager's PIN or an inbox approval. The amounts are limits in Permissions and limits, which the owner can change per role for the group.** Why: the limit decides who signs, not whether the owner sees it. One change to the design: its "within the ₦5,000.00 tolerance · supervisor sign-off needed" reads as both; within the limit the reason is enough. Per-hotel amounts would be a new kind of setting that no other limit has; add it only if a pilot asks.
4. **Can front desk agents run a shift, or only cashiers?** Recommended: **both, by permission: front desk agents, cashiers, F&B managers, GMs, finance and the owner can open, count and close their own shift (as the permissions matrix already says).** Why: in most Nigerian hotels the front desk agent is the cashier; a separate cashier role at the desk is a large-hotel luxury, and the control is the blind count and the name on it, not the job title.
5. **Can a submitted count be reopened?** (The design says "only a supervisor can reopen the count, with a reason.") Recommended: **no, not in Stage 1: once submitted, the count stands. A miscount is explained in the reason, and a large one needs a manager's PIN, who can recount with the cashier before signing; the confirm before submitting shows every figure once more.** Why: once the expected figure is on screen, a recount is no longer blind, and a reopen button is the one way to turn a short into "balanced" with nobody's name on the difference. If pilots need it, a later brick can add a witnessed recount that keeps both counts.

**Legal checks (not owner decisions):** none new. Shifts hold no guest personal data (names of staff only; no guest names in approval subjects). The NGN note list (`NGN_NOTES`: ₦1,000, ₦500, ₦200, ₦100, ₦50, then smaller notes and coins as one amount) should be checked against the notes the CBN currently issues before the pilot; it's a constant, not a statutory rule. FX rates are brick 3's CBN rates.
