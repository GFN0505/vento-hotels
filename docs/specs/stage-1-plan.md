# Stage 1 plan: front office

- **Status:** approved (owner, 2026-11-26)
- **Goal (from `docs/PRODUCT.md`):** a pilot hotel runs its front desk without its old PMS: rooms, manual rates, reservations, tape chart, check-in/out, walk-ins, folios, payments, cashier shifts, corporate accounts with credit limits and settle-to-company, night audit, guest register, core reports, owner's daily report, data import.
- **Rule:** one brick = one spec = one pull request, in this order, each ending usable and tested on the demo seed. Everything sits behind the `front_desk` switch (decision 0021); the pilot gets it when brick 17 lands, switched on from the Vento console (brick 5b).

**Order notes.** 1b comes before every brick that adds a page, so reservations, the tape chart, folios, shifts and reports are built to the hotel-switch rule from their first commit (owner, 2026-11-26). 1–3 are setup and unblock everything that prices or counts rooms. 4 before 5: discounts within the limit work in 4, over-limit ones wait for 5. 9 before 10 so payments always land on an open shift. 13 needs 10 and 12. 15–16 read frozen night-audit data, so they follow 13. Import is last because it reuses every service before it; until then hotels use the builders. 5b (the Vento console) follows 5 so the owner's consent can use the PIN approval from 5, and comes this early because every customer procedure must declare read or write for the support view, which is cheapest while there are few of them; it must land before 17, since going live needs the console to switch `front_desk` on for the pilot and the onboarding specialist's support access (owner, 2026-11-27, decision 0027).

**Stage 1 is done when** `front-desk-morning`, `night-audit`, `owner-morning` and `going-live` pass their Stage 1 steps, and golden-thread hops 1, 4, 8 and 9 pass. Room status board, discrepancy checks and work orders stay in Stage 2; the rates grid, channels and guest profiles beyond what check-in needs stay in Stage 4.
