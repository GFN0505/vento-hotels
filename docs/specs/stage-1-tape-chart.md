## Problem
The desk can book, change and cancel, but it can't see the house. Nobody can answer "which room is Mr Mensah in?", "is 502 free on Saturday if he stays two more nights?" or "can we take 503 off for the AC repair without leaving a guest without a room?" without a paper chart or the old PMS. Rooms are only counted per type, so a booking has no room until someone writes one on a sheet, two agents promise the same room, and an out-of-order room is still counted as sellable, so the last "free" Executive King is one the guest can't sleep in. And when housekeeping calls "502 is ready", there's nowhere to record it, so the desk sends an early arrival up to a room that hasn't been turned. Every one of these is a moment where a room night is lost or given away and nobody can say who decided it.

Dozens of times a shift, often with a queue.

- **Owner** (Mrs Adaeze Okafor): next morning, sees every room taken off sale with dates, reason and name (brick 16), and occupancy that excludes those rooms (bricks 15–16).

## Success looks like
- The tape chart for 120 rooms × 14 days shows something useful in **under 1 second** on the desk PC, and a full 30-day view in under 2 seconds.
- Assign or move a booking to another room of the same type in **1 drag** (or **3 clicks** from the reservation: Room move → room → Move), with Undo.
- Extend a stay by dragging its end in **2 actions** (drag, Extend stay) with the price shown before saving.
- Mark a room clean in **2 clicks** (room → Mark clean), offline too.
- Put a room out of order in **5 clicks**, and **zero** out-of-order periods that leave a booking without a room: refused while a booking is assigned to the room, or when a night would be short (tests).
- **Zero** rooms with two bookings on the same night, and **zero** bookings in a room that's out of order (database constraint plus the service check under the room lock; 200-run race tests).
- **Zero** upgrades at the booked price above the agent's discount limit without an approval; every one is in the owner's daily report with names.
- After every move, extend, out-of-order change and booking: rooms left per type per night = active rooms − active rooms out of order that night − booked, never below 0 except where the overbooking limit allows (invariant test).

## Acceptance criteria

### Main path
**What this brick delivers**
1. The **tape chart** page: rooms × dates for one hotel, stays as bars, out-of-order blocks, availability per type per night, unassigned bookings per type.
2. **Room assignment and moves** for `confirmed` stays: assign, move (same type: no inventory change; another type: brick 6's modify with a room), unassign, with Undo for same-type changes. The Room move dialog (#31) from the chart and from the reservation detail.
3. **Drag to extend or shorten** a stay's departure, through brick 6's change service.
4. **Room status**: Mark clean / Mark dirty, offline too.
5. **Out of order**: put a room out of order for dates with a reason, change the dates, return it to service; rooms left drops for those nights under the inventory lock.
6. **Phone** (below `md`): a one-day list by room type with the same actions, no dragging.
7. Demo seed: assigned rooms, room statuses, 503 out of order, and situations to try every rule below.

**The chart**
- **Window:** starts 2 days before the business date (as the design: past nights for context, read-only), 7, 14 (default) or 30 days; **Previous week** / **Next week**, **Today** (T) back to the default window; **New reservation** (N). The chosen range is remembered per browser.
- **Rows:** active rooms grouped by room type (the type's sort order), then floor, then number. Archived rooms and types aren't shown. Each type block: header (name, tonight's Best available price "excl. taxes", or nothing when not priced), the **Unassigned** lane (only when the type has unassigned bookings in the window), its rooms, then **Available** (rooms left per night from the inventory, as on New reservation; rose at 0).
- **Room label:** number, status badge (Inspected with a check icon, Clean, Dirty, Out of order), icons for accessible and connecting rooms with a tooltip ("Connects to 402"). `aria-label` "Room 502, Executive King, dirty".
- **Bars:** `confirmed` stays in brand colour; text "Mensah D." and, if room, "· 3 nights"; tooltip and `aria-label` "Daniel Mensah, Executive King, 25–28 Nov 2026, 3 nights, Room 502". Cancelled and no-show stays aren't drawn (they hold no room). In-house, checked-out and tentative colours come with bricks 8, 10 and 14; the legend shows only what exists. Bars starting before the window show a left arrow.
- **Out of order:** rose hatched block with "Out of order · AC" and the note in the tooltip; past blocks muted.
- **Unassigned lane:** collapsed it shows a count per night; clicking a count (or the lane name) expands it to the bookings as bars, stacked, ready to drag. Expanded state is remembered per type for the session.
- **Filters:** pills **All room types** / one type, **Floor**, **Unassigned** (count). Filtered empty: "No rooms match these filters." → **Clear filters**. Header line "23 Nov – 6 Dec 2026 · 17 rooms shown of 120".
- **Click** on a bar opens a small card: guest, reservation number, type, plan, dates, room, deposit due, with **Open reservation**, **Room move** (M), **Modify** (opens brick 6's dialog). Click on an empty cell of a room on or after the business date opens **New reservation** with the room type, arrival = that night, departure +1, and the room ("Room 506 will be assigned"). Click on a room label opens the **room menu**: **Mark clean** or **Mark dirty**, **Put out of order** (or **Change dates** and **Back in service** when one is active or upcoming), **History** (brick 1's room audit drawer).
- **Keyboard:** arrows move the focus cell; Enter opens what's there (card, New reservation, room menu); on a focused bar **M** opens Room move, **E** opens Modify; Escape cancels a drag. Every drag has this keyboard or click alternative (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.7).
- **Sticky** room column and date header; the chart scrolls inside its own container; no page scroll sideways.
- **Freshness:** refetched on focus and every 30 seconds while visible; after any change made here, at once. A drop on stale data is checked by the server and refused with the current state ("402 was just taken for 26 Nov.").
- **Size:** rows are virtualised above 150 rooms (hotels of 200+ rooms are the design's revision trigger).

**Assign and move (stays `confirmed`, arrival on or after the business date)**
- **Who:** `room.move`. **What can be moved:** a `confirmed` stay whose arrival is on or after the business date. A stay whose arrival has passed (waiting to be marked no-show) can't be moved: "Arrival was 24 Nov 2026. Mark it as a no-show." In-house moves come with brick 8 (below).
- **The room must be free every night of the stay:** active, of a type that fits the party (max adults and children), no other `confirmed` (or, from brick 8, `checked_in`) stay assigned to it on any of those nights, and no out-of-order period on any of them.
- **Same room type:** changes the stay's room only. No inventory row changes, no price change, no approval. Runs immediately with **Undo** (8 seconds); Undo moves it back if the old room is still free ("404 was just taken, so Ngozi Okafor stays in 401.").
- **Another room type:** a **modify** through brick 6's change service with the target room: the change quotes on drop (popover) or in the dialog; nothing is saved until **Move to 506**. Every night is priced as brick 6 prices a room type change (same plan, the new type's price now), unless **Keep ₦168,000.00 a night** is chosen: then each night keeps its old sold price and the difference to the new type's plan price is a **discount** with reason **Upgrade** (Open question 2), measured per night against the mover's `stay.rate_override` limit; over it, **Ask for approval · 26.32%** (kind `modify_discount`, as brick 6). The type must be sold by the stay's plan ("Bed & breakfast isn't sold for Junior Suite."), have the nights bookable, and fit the party. A move to a cheaper type after the free-cancellation deadline shows brick 6's late-change penalty before saving.
- **Default rate on a move to another type** is **Charge {type} rate** (design shows Keep; see design changes): giving the better room away is a choice someone makes and is named for, never the default.
- **Unassign:** drag a bar onto its type's Unassigned lane, or **Remove room** in the Room move dialog. Same type only, Undo.
- **Reason:** optional before arrival (pills **Guest request**, **Maintenance**, **Upgrade**, **Noise**, **Other** with text) (Open question 3); required when keeping the price on an upgrade (it's a discount reason).
- **Room move dialog** (from the card, the reservation detail's **Room move**, M, or the phone): title "Room move · Ngozi Okafor" (or "Assign room · Funmilayo Adebayo" when none); subtitle "404 Deluxe King · 25–27 Nov 2026 · 2 nights". Lists rooms free for every night: same type first, ready rooms (Inspected, then Clean) first, then Dirty, then other types the plan sells and the party fits, cheapest first, with "+₦60,000.00 a night excl. taxes" or "−₦30,000.00 a night excl. taxes". Status badges show only when the arrival is the business date (a dirty room matters today, not for 3 Dec). Up to 8 rooms per type with **Show all 23**. Room features shown ("City view", "Quiet side", "Connects to 402"). Primary **Move to 401** / **Assign 408**.
- **Book from a cell:** New reservation (brick 4) carries the room; booking assigns it in the booking's transaction. If it was taken meanwhile: "Room 506 was just taken for 26 Nov. Book without a room, or pick another." with **Book without a room**.
- **Modify (brick 6) keeps the room** when it is free on every new night; otherwise the Modify dialog says before saving "Room 502 isn't free on 28 Nov (Kalu E.). The booking will be unassigned." A room type change through Modify unassigns the room (it's another type's room) unless made by dropping on a room. Cancel and no-show free the room (the stay keeps its room for history; it no longer blocks it).
- **In-house moves (brick 8, same service and dialog):** a `checked_in` stay moves from the business date on; nights already closed by night audit keep their room; the reason is required; the old room becomes `dirty`; "Keep / Charge new room rate" applies to the remaining nights only. Correcting the room of a night already audited isn't offered in Stage 1.

**Drag to extend or shorten**
- The right end of a `confirmed` bar on or after the business date has a handle; dragging it changes the **departure** only (arrival changes go through Modify). Snaps to nights; at least 1 night.
- On release, the popover calls `reservations.quoteChange` and shows brick 6's figures: the new nights (or the nights dropped), tax lines, **Added to stay +₦441,000.00** or **Taken off the stay −₦220,500.00**, the late-change penalty when it applies, and the room check ("Room 502 is free both nights."). **Extend stay** / **Shorten stay** saves through `reservations.modify` with the `changeHash` shown. Over-limit discounts carried to new nights follow brick 6 (Ask for approval, or Price new nights without the discount).
- **Room not free on the new nights:** the popover says "Room 502 is booked from 28 Nov (Kalu E.). Move the stay to a free room first, or extend in Modify and leave it unassigned." and offers **Room move**; nothing is saved.
- Unassigned bars (expanded lane) extend the same way without the room check.

**Room status (minimal, Stage 1)**
- **Mark clean** sets `clean`; **Mark dirty** sets `dirty`. Allowed on any room whatever its stays or out-of-order state (cleanliness is separate from sellability). `inspected` is shown but only set by inspections (Stage 2) and brick 1's set-up action. Undo (8 seconds) puts back the previous status.
- The room records who and when: the label tooltip and room menu read "Clean · Grace Akpan · 11:10".
- Nothing changes status automatically in this brick. Check-out sets `dirty` (bricks 8/10); night audit (brick 13) sets out-of-order rooms whose period ended to `dirty`; housekeeping rules (stayovers) are Stage 2.

**Out of order**
- **Who:** `room.out_of_order` (Open question 1).
- **Fields:** **From** (on or after the business date; default the business date), **Back in service** (the first night the room can be sold again, like a departure; default From + 1; at most 365 nights), **Reason** pill **AC**, **Plumbing**, **Electrical**, **Renovation**, **Other** (text required), **Note** (optional, 200 characters). The dialog shows "2 nights".
- **Checks, live as the dates change, and again under lock on save:**
  1. No `confirmed` (or `checked_in`) stay assigned to the room on those nights: "Room 503 has Emeka Kalu on 25–27 Nov. Move the booking first." with **Move Emeka Kalu**.
  2. No night would hold more bookings than rooms plus the overbooking limit (0 in Stage 1), counting unassigned bookings: "Executive King would be 1 room short on 26 Nov. Move or cancel a booking of that type, or choose other dates." Refused, never allowed past the limit (non-negotiable 4). The dialog names the type's bookings that night as a link to the chart filtered to it.
  3. No overlap with another out-of-order period of the room: "503 is already out of order 26–28 Nov. Change those dates instead."
- **Save:** one period; rooms left for the room's type drops by one on each night, on every screen at once. If From is the business date, the room is also set `dirty` (repairs make mess; it comes back to be cleaned).
- **Change dates:** an upcoming period's From and Back in service, a current period's Back in service (its start is history); extending runs the same checks for the added nights; shortening frees them. **Back in service** on a current period ends it from the business date (Back in service = business date) and asks "Room is" **Dirty** (default) / **Clean**; on an upcoming period it reads **Remove** ("Remove the out-of-order for 1–3 Dec?"). A period whose dates have passed is read-only.
- Reason required for every change; "Recorded with your name."

### Audit events
All with `recordAudit` in the action's transaction: actor, device, property, business date, before/after, reason, approver.

- `room.status_changed` (brick 1's event): before and after `housekeepingStatus`, `source` (`chart`, `day_list`, `out_of_order`, `back_in_service`, `undo`), `occurredAt` (device time) and `syncedAt` when it came from the offline outbox.
- `room.out_of_order`: period id, room (number, type), from, back in service, nights, reason, text, note, `liveProperty`, `exception: 'out_of_order'` when live.
- `room.out_of_order_changed`: before and after dates, nights added and removed, reason; `exception: 'out_of_order'` only when nights are added on a live hotel.
- `room.back_in_service`: period id, ended from (business date) or removed, nights returned, status chosen, reason.

### Leakage exceptions
- **Out of order** (new exception type, Open question 1): every period put on, and every extension, on a live hotel. Owner's daily report line (brick 16): "Out of order · Room 503 Executive King · 25–27 Nov 2026 · 2 nights · AC: compressor fault, part needed · Tunde Bello". An extension: "Out of order extended · Room 503 · +1 night to 28 Nov · Tunde Bello". Returning a room early isn't an exception. Before go-live: `liveProperty: false`, not reported. Why: a room taken off the system and sold for cash is the oldest leakage in Nigerian hotels; naming it every morning is the control, and Stage 2's room checks catch a sleeper in it.
- **Discount** (existing): an upgrade at the booked price is a discount with reason **Upgrade**: "Discount 26.32% · Upgrade · Ngozi Okafor · Deluxe King → Executive King · Chiamaka Eze · Approved by Tunde Bello".
- Assignments, same-type moves and clean/dirty changes are audited facts, not exceptions. Room discrepancies (sleeper, skipper) are Stage 2.

### Offline behaviour
- **Readable offline:** the chart (and the phone day list) from the last load, under the offline banner, with "Last updated 14:32".
- **Works offline:** **Mark clean** and **Mark dirty** (decision 0009's allow-list): applied at once with a "Will sync" mark, queued in the outbox with an idempotency key and the device time; synced in order. If two changes to one room conflict, the later by device time wins and the earlier is kept in history (both audited); a change older than the room's last status change on the server isn't applied and shows in the sync queue: "502's status changed to dirty by Grace Akpan at 11:20 after your change at 11:05. Kept as dirty." A status change on a room put out of order meanwhile still applies (cleanliness is separate).
- **Blocked offline** (need live rooms and inventory): assign, move, unassign, drag to extend, book from a cell, out of order. Bars aren't draggable; the actions are disabled with "Moving rooms needs a connection." / "Out of order needs a connection." An open dialog keeps its fields and disables its button with the same words. Nothing is queued.
- A request sent just before the connection dropped is retried with the same key when it returns, if the dialog or popover is still open ("Checking whether Ngozi Okafor was moved…"); never twice.

### Business date
- The chart's highlighted column, "today" in the day list, the earliest night a bar can be dropped or extended into, the earliest out-of-order From, and what "current" and "upcoming" mean for a period are all the **business date**, not the clock. After midnight before night audit, 25 Nov is still today; the top bar shows the business date in amber (existing).
- Every assignment, move, status change and out-of-order change records the hotel's business date when it happened.
- After night audit (brick 13): the window moves on; a period that ended on the new business date's eve leaves the room `dirty`; nights before the business date are read-only on the chart.

### Availability locking
- **Rooms left** = active rooms of the type − active rooms of the type out of order that night − booked; **bookable** = rooms left + the overbooking limit. `capacityOf` becomes per night (counted live after the inventory lock, as now). New reservation, Modify, the rates calendar's rooms left and the chart all use it.
- **Lock order** (`apps/api/src/reservations/locks.ts`, unchanged steps, new users):
  - **Same-type assign, move, unassign:** hotel row FOR SHARE (2) → reservation row FOR UPDATE (2b) → room rows FOR UPDATE, old and new in id order (3). No inventory rows.
  - **Move to another type, drag-extend with a room:** brick 6's change transaction with the target room locked at step 3, between 2b and the inventory rows (4) for the union of old and new nights, then approvals (5).
  - **Book from a cell:** rates lock (1, shared) → hotel (2) → the room (3) → inventory (4) → … as brick 4.
  - **Out of order (create, change, end):** hotel FOR SHARE (2) → the room FOR UPDATE (3) → inventory rows of its type for the nights affected (4). Under the locks: the assigned-stay check (safe: every assignment locks the room row), the overlap check, then for every added night bookable ≥ 0 after removing the room (`OOO_WOULD_OVERBOOK` otherwise).
  - **Room count changes (brick 1)** already take 2 → 3 → 4; they now also refuse to archive a room or change its type while it has assigned future stays ("Room 404 has a booking on 26 Nov. Move it first.") or an active or upcoming out-of-order period ("End 503's out-of-order first."), and their shortfall check counts out-of-order rooms.
- No cycle: bookings and changes take 3 only for the one target room and after 2b; room status changes lock only the room row (3) and nothing after.
- **Database constraints (last line of defence):** no two `confirmed` or `checked_in` stays with the same room on overlapping nights (exclusion constraint on `room_id` and `daterange(arrival, departure)` where active; needs `btree_gist`), and no two overlapping out-of-order periods per room. Stay vs out-of-order overlap is checked by the service under the room lock (cross-table).
- **Tests (200 runs each):** two agents assign different stays to the last free Deluxe King room for a night: one wins, the other `ROOM_TAKEN`. Out of order on 503 races a booking of the last Executive King on 26 Nov: exactly one succeeds. Out of order races an assignment to the same room: one wins. Drag-extend into 502 races an assignment of another stay to 502 on the new night: one wins. A move Deluxe King → Executive King races a booking Executive King → Deluxe King: no deadlocks. Counter invariant after random concurrent books, modifies, moves, cancels and out-of-order changes: booked = active stay nights per row, and booked ≤ rooms − out of order + limit on every row the operations touched.

### Empty, error and edge cases
- **No rooms:** "No rooms at Kora Victoria Island yet. Rooms are set up in Rates & channels." → **Set up rooms** (for `rooms.setup`; others: "Ask your GM to set up rooms.").
- **No bookings in the window:** the chart shows rooms and Available with no bars; a quiet line under the header "No bookings 23 Nov – 6 Dec 2026." with **New reservation**.
- **Loading:** a skeleton of the grid (type headers, room column, 14 columns); **error:** "The tape chart didn't load. Try again." with **Try again**.
- **Room taken meanwhile:** "402 was just taken for 26 Nov." (`ROOM_TAKEN`), the bar snaps back, the chart refreshes.
- **Room out of order on a night:** "503 is out of order 25–27 Nov." (`ROOM_OUT_OF_ORDER`). **Too small:** "506 sleeps 2 adults and 1 child." (`ROOM_TOO_SMALL`). **Archived room:** "Room 412 was removed." (`ROOM_ARCHIVED`).
- **Stay changed meanwhile:** brick 6's `RESERVATION_CHANGED` ("Sade Bakare changed this booking at 11:08. Check the new details."); cancelled meanwhile: "Chiamaka Eze cancelled this booking at 11:20."
- **Not movable:** cancelled, no-show, arrival passed (`NOT_MOVABLE` with the reason as above).
- **Out of order:** `OOO_ROOM_HAS_BOOKINGS`, `OOO_WOULD_OVERBOOK`, `OOO_OVERLAPS`, `OOO_PAST_DATE` ("Out of order can start on 25 Nov 2026 at the earliest."), `OOO_TOO_LONG` ("Out of order can last up to 365 nights."), `OOO_REASON_REQUIRED` ("Pick a reason."), `OOO_ENDED` ("This out-of-order already ended on 24 Nov 2026.").
- **Party doesn't fit any free room of the type:** the dialog says "No Deluxe King room is free for all of 25–27 Nov. 3 Executive King rooms are free." (split stays across rooms aren't offered).
- **Overbooked night (Stage 4 OTA, or limit > 0):** Available shows "−1" in rose with tooltip "1 more booking than rooms"; the Unassigned lane can't be cleared that night; nothing here creates it.
- **Room with an out-of-order period and a dirty status:** label shows Out of order; the menu still offers Mark clean.
- **Long names, 30 days, 1-night stays:** bars show the surname only below 2 nights wide, full in the tooltip and card.
- **Idempotency key reused with a different action:** "This change was already saved." with the result.
- **Archived plan after booking:** a move to another type needs new nights priced on the plan: brick 6's "Bed & breakfast was archived, so new nights can't be priced." Same-type moves are unaffected.

## Behind the scenes
- **Folio postings:** none. Folios belong to the stay, not the room, so moves never touch them (brick 10).
- **Night audit (brick 13):** available room nights exclude rooms out of order that night; a room whose out-of-order period ended is set `dirty` (`room.status_changed`, source `back_in_service`); no check blocks on unassigned arrivals (check-in assigns). A pre-check lists arrivals for the business date without a room, as information.
- **Ledger:** none.
- **Jobs:** none new. No ARI push until channels (Stage 4), which read the same rooms-left after commit.
- **Notifications:** in app only: brick 5's bell for upgrade approvals. No guest messages (brick 8's "room ready" message is Stage 4).
- **Data import (brick 17):** future reservations keep the room from the old PMS when it is free for every night (through the assign service, same checks); otherwise they come in unassigned and the import lists them ("12 bookings came in without a room: their room was already taken or doesn't exist"). Out-of-order rooms at go-live are entered by hand (few). Room statuses at go-live: brick 1's "Mark inspected" before go-live, then Mark dirty per room (Open questions in `going-live.md`).
- **Stage 2:** the room status board, attendant app and inspections write the same `housekeepingStatus` through the same service; work orders create out-of-order periods through the same service with the work order linked; room checks compare against the assigned rooms.
- **Code layout:** `apps/api/src/rooms/status/` (mark clean/dirty, offline command handler), `apps/api/src/rooms/out-of-order/` (quote, create, change, end), `apps/api/src/reservations/assign/` (room options, assign, move, unassign), `apps/api/src/tape-chart/` (the one read); `capacityOf` per night in `reservations/locks.ts`; the change service gains `roomId` and `keepBookedPrice`. Pure helpers in `@vento/shared`: bar geometry (nights in window), free-room checks for the dialog, out-of-order night counts. ## Open questions for the owner (answered)

All four answered as recommended (owner, 2026-11-27): out of order by GM, housekeeping supervisor, maintenance and owner / group admin (not the front desk), each period a line in the owner's report; an upgrade at the booked price is a discount with reason Upgrade; a move before arrival needs no reason (in house it always does); 503 is the only room out of order on 25 Nov in the demo.

1. **Who can put a room out of order, and does the owner see it every morning?** Recommended: **GM, housekeeping supervisor, maintenance and owner / group admin, not the front desk; every out-of-order period or extension on a live hotel is a line in the owner's daily report with room, dates, reason and name.** Why: taking a room off the system and selling it for cash is the oldest leakage in a Nigerian hotel, so the people selling rooms shouldn't also hide them, and the owner should see every room night taken off sale.
2. **Is an upgrade at the booked price a discount?** Recommended: **yes: keeping the cheaper price in a better room is a discount against the better room's price, with reason Upgrade, within the mover's discount limit or with a manager's PIN, and in the owner's report.** Why: a free upgrade gives away exactly what a discount does, and without this an agent could move a friend into a suite at a Standard price with nobody's name on it.
3. **Does moving a booking before arrival need a reason?** Recommended: **no: before arrival the reason is optional and a same-type move has Undo; once the guest is in house (brick 8) a reason is always required.** Why: juggling rooms before arrival is routine and costs nothing; asking for a reason on every drag slows the desk without catching leakage, which shows up in-house and in Stage 2's room checks.
4. **Which rooms are out of order on 25 Nov in the demo?** Recommended: **503 only (as the walkthroughs), with the design's 406 shown as a block that ended the day before and 305 as an upcoming block on 1–3 Dec; Mr Mensah, not "Whitfield J.", is the bar on 502.** Why: night audit, the owner's report and the golden thread all rest on one room out of order ("85 of 119", "Room 503 out of order until 27 Nov"), while the design's names are sample data; this keeps both the figures and the design's look.

**Legal checks (not owner decisions):** none new. Moves and out-of-order periods collect no personal data; reasons and notes are internal and never sent to processors. Tax lines in the popovers come from brick 3's rules, still To verify under the 2025 tax acts before the pilot.

## Build notes (brick 7, as built)
Small calls made while building, for the reviewer and the owner:
- **Procedure names:** as the spec's table, with room status at `rooms.setStatus` and out of order at `rooms.outOfOrder.check / create / change / end`. Moves to another room type, drag-extends and bookings from a cell go through `reservations.modify` and `reservations.create` with `roomId`; `stays.assignRoom` refuses a room of another type (`ROOM_WRONG_TYPE`), because the price changes.
- **A same-type assignment is a `reservation_changes` row of kind `room`**: its key makes a resend return the first result, and it raises the stay's version, so a Modify dialog opened before the move says who changed the booking.
- **The type header price** is tonight's price on the hotel's base plan (the first own-price plan open to anyone, as the Rooms page's Base rate), date prices included.
- **Upgrade at the booked price on a stay with several nights at different prices:** each night keeps its own sold price, and the discount recorded on the stay (and checked against the limit) is the largest per-night %, so no night goes past the limit unseen.
- **Room move dialog's price difference** is the plan's average price a night for the other type minus the stay's type over the stay's nights, excl. taxes; the exact figures come from the change quote when a type is picked.
- **Out of order after it started:** Change dates keeps a current period's From (its start is history); Back in service on a period that starts today removes it (nothing was out of order yet) and still sets the room's status as asked. Every change or end needs a reason ("Recorded with your name."); the period's version answers "This out-of-order changed meanwhile. Check the new dates." when two people edit it.
- **"Ask Tunde Bello"** in the out-of-order refusal is "Ask your GM." (the server doesn't know who the hotel's GM is by name).
- **Room status from an offline device:** a change older than the room's last one on the server is kept as a `superseded` receipt and a `room.status_changed` event with `applied: false`, and the sync queue shows the spec's sentence. A device clock more than 5 minutes ahead is treated as now.
- **The chart's 30-second refresh** doesn't count as activity on a shared device (`tapeChart.get` is a background read), so a PIN session left on the chart still locks after its idle time.
