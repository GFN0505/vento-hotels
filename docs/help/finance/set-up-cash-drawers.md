# Set up cash drawers

A cash drawer is a named place where a hotel's cash is kept at the desk, like **Desk 1** or **Desk 2**, with the POS terminals at it. Every cashier shift is opened on one drawer. Every hotel starts with Desk 1. Finance and the owner set drawers up in **Settings › Payments**, under **Cash drawers**; GMs can read them.

## Add or change a drawer
1. Press **Add drawer**.
2. Give it a **Name** staff use, for example "Desk 2" (up to 30 characters, different from the hotel's other drawers).
3. Tick the **POS terminals at this drawer**. A terminal can sit at more than one drawer.
4. Press **Add drawer**. On a live hotel, give a reason; it shows in the owner's daily report.

Click a drawer to rename it or change its terminals. Each drawer shows who has it now ("Shift 1 open · Chiamaka Eze") or what was left in it ("Free · ₦50,000.00 float").

## Archive a drawer
Open it and press **Archive drawer**, with a reason. A drawer with a shift open can't be archived until the shift closes, and a hotel always keeps at least one active drawer. Any float left in an archived drawer isn't moved anywhere: take it to the safe. Restore an archived drawer from the **Archived** list.

Archiving a POS terminal takes it off every drawer; shifts already open keep counting it.

Setting up drawers needs your own sign-in (not a PIN on a shared device) and a connection.
