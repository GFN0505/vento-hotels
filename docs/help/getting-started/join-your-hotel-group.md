# Join your hotel group

When your hotel invites you, you get an email titled "… invited you to … on Vento Hotels".

1. Open the link in the email. It shows your group, your role, the hotels you'll see, and when the invite expires.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Create a password: at least 8 characters with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a special character. Type it again to confirm.
4. Click **Accept and continue**. You're signed in and see your hotels.

You don't need to confirm your email separately: the invite link already did that.

## If you already use Vento
The link asks for your password instead. Sign in and you're added to the group.

## If the link doesn't work
Invites last 7 days and work once. If the page says the invite can't be used, ask the person who invited you to resend it. If you're signed in as someone else, click **Sign out and continue**.
