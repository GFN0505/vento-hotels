# Register a shared device

Do this once for each tablet, PC or phone your staff share. It takes about a minute. You need to be an owner, group admin or someone allowed to manage people.

1. On the device itself, open Vento and sign in with your email and password.
2. Go to **Settings** › **Devices** › **Register this device**.
3. Give it a name staff will recognise, for example POS-VI-03, and choose what it's for: front desk PC, restaurant & bar tablet or housekeeping phone. If you have several hotels, choose which one.
4. Click **Register**, then **Lock this device**.

The device now shows the lock screen with everyone at that hotel who has set a PIN.

## Rename or remove a device
In **Settings** › **Devices**, click **Rename**, or **Revoke** for a lost or retired device. Revoking needs a reason. Everyone on it is signed out at once and its lock screen stops working.

Every registration, rename and revoke is recorded in the audit log with your name.
