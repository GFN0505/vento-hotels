# Turn on two-step sign-in

A password alone can be stolen. With two-step sign-in, Vento also asks for a 6-digit code from an app on your phone, so a stolen password can't get in on its own. Owners and managers should turn it on; it takes about two minutes.

## Turn it on
1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.
2. In Vento, click your initials (top right) › **Two-step sign-in**, and confirm your password.
3. Scan the QR code with the app (or type the key shown under it). The app now shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
4. Type the code into Vento. It checks itself when you've typed all six digits.
5. Save your 10 backup codes: **Download** or **Copy** them and keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone. Each code signs you in once if you lose your phone.

Turning it on signs you out of Vento everywhere else, in case your password was already in use somewhere.

## Signing in
After your password, enter the 6-digit code from the app. Tick **Don't ask again on this computer for 30 days** on your own computer, never on a shared one.

## Lost your phone?
Choose **Use a backup code instead** on the code screen. Once you're in, turn two-step sign-in off and on again with your new phone.

## Turn it off
Click your initials › **Two-step sign-in** › confirm your password. Computers you'd trusted are forgotten.
