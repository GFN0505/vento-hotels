# Preview what each role sees

Owners and group admins can see Vento exactly as someone in another role would: which menus they have and which pages they can open. Useful before inviting your team, or when someone says "I can't find it".

1. Click your initials (top right) › **Preview as a role**.
2. Choose a role, for example **Waiter / bartender**, and click **Preview as Waiter / bartender**.
3. An amber bar across the top shows you're previewing. Browse the menu and pages.
4. Click **Stop previewing** to go back to your own view.

The preview changes what you see, not what you can do: anything you save still uses your own access. What each role may do, and its limits, is set in **Settings** › **Permissions and limits**.
