# Manage your companies

Companies are the legal entities in your group that own your hotels. Invoices, VAT and the books use the company that owns each hotel. Onboarding only asks for a company's name, so complete its RC number, TIN and VAT registration here. You need to be the owner or a group admin.

## Complete a company's details
1. Go to **Settings** › **Companies**, and click the company. (From a hotel's settings, **Edit company** opens the company that owns it.)
2. Type the **RC number** as on the CAC certificate, for example RC 1184402 or BN 3456789. Just the digits works too: Vento shows how it will save it ("Saved as RC 1184402").
3. Type the **TIN**, for example 21847390-0001.
4. Choose whether the company is **VAT registered**.
5. Click **Save company**.

A company without an RC number or TIN shows **RC number and TIN missing** in the list and on the settings of every hotel it owns.

Two companies in your group can't share a name, an RC number or a TIN. If you type one that's already used, Vento says which company has it.

Once a hotel the company owns is live, changing its TIN or VAT registration asks for a reason. It's recorded with your name and shows in the owner's daily report. A change of VAT registration then starts on the next business date, so a day's charges never mix two rules: until then the company shows, for example, "Not VAT registered from 27 Nov 2026". Choose the current answer again to cancel it.

## Add a company
Click **Add a company**, type its legal name (as on the CAC certificate) and any details you have, then click **Save company**. You can also add one while adding a hotel: choose **Add a company…** at the end of the **Owning company** list.

## Archive and restore a company
Open the company and click **Archive company** at the foot of the sheet, then give a reason. A company that still owns hotels can't be archived: Vento lists them so you can move them to another company first (in each hotel's settings, under **Owning company**).

Archived companies are under **Archived**, with **Restore**.

## Which companies you see
With **All hotels** chosen in the hotel switch at the top, you see every company. With one hotel chosen, you see the company that owns it.

If a company also owns hotels you don't have access to, you can see it but not change it. Ask the owner.

## Offline
Changing companies needs a connection. Offline, the page shows what was saved on your device, read-only.
