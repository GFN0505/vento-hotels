# Sign in on a shared device with your PIN

Restaurant tablets, front desk PCs and housekeeping phones are shared. Instead of typing your email and password, you tap your name and enter your 4-digit staff PIN.

## Set your PIN (once)
1. Sign in with your email and password, on your own phone or any device.
2. Open the menu under your initials (top right) › **Set staff PIN**.
3. Enter 4 digits twice, then your password, and click **Save PIN**. Avoid 1234 or one digit repeated.

## Sign in
1. On the shared device, tap your name.
2. Enter your PIN. You're in.

You only see your own hotel on a shared device. Settings like people and permissions need your email and password on your own device.

## Signing out
Tap **Lock** at the top when you step away. The device also signs you out by itself: after 90 seconds without a touch on restaurant tablets, and 5 minutes on front desk PCs and housekeeping phones.

## If it doesn't work
- **Wrong PIN:** you have 5 tries. After that your PIN is locked for 15 minutes, or a manager can reset it.
- **Forgot your PIN:** ask a manager to reset it (Members and roles › Edit › Reset PIN), then set a new one.
- **Your name isn't there:** you haven't set a PIN yet, or you don't work at this device's hotel.
