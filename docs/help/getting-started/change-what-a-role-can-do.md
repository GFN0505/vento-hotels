# Change what a role can do

Every group starts with Vento's defaults for what each role may do and how far, for example front desk discounts of up to 10%. Owners can change any role except Owner.

1. Open **Settings** › **Permissions and limits**. On a computer you see every role side by side; choose **By role** (or use a phone) to see one role at a time.
2. Click the rule you want to change (on a phone, **Change** next to it).
3. Tick or untick **Allowed**. For rules with a limit, enter the most that role may give, as a percentage or in naira. Leave it empty for no limit.
4. Give a **Reason**. It's saved with your name in the audit log.
5. Click **Save rule**.

Rules you've changed from Vento's defaults are marked with an amber dot. Above a limit, staff need a manager with a higher limit to approve.

Group admins and others who manage people can see this page but can't change it.
