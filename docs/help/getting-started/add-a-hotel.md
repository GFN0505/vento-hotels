# Add a hotel

Each hotel in your group has its own settings: its name, logo, address, check-in and check-out times, and the company that owns it. Add a new hotel in under two minutes, complete the details onboarding left out, or archive a hotel that has closed. You need to be the owner or a group admin. A GM can change their own hotel's check-in and check-out times.

## Complete your first hotel
Onboarding only asks for your hotel's name and state. Registration cards, invoices and guest messages need its full address.
1. On **Home**, click **Complete** beside **Complete hotel and company details**, or go to **Settings** › **Hotels**.
2. Type the **Street** and **Area**, choose the **State**, then the **LGA** (the LGA list follows the state you choose).
3. Click **Save changes**.

To add a logo, click **Upload** under **Hotel logo** and choose a PNG or JPEG of up to 2 MB, at least 200 px on each side. It's used on invoices, receipts, the booking engine and the guest app, not on Vento's own screens. **Remove** takes it off, with **Undo** for a few seconds.

## Add a hotel
1. Go to **Settings** › **Hotels** and click **Add a hotel**.
2. Type the hotel's name. The **Owning company** is already chosen (the company that owns most of your hotels); pick another, or choose **Add a company…** at the end of the list to add one without leaving.
3. Type the street and area, choose the state and LGA, and check the check-in and check-out times (14:00 and 12:00 to start). The time zone is under **More options**; Nigerian hotels keep Africa/Lagos.
4. Click **Add hotel**.

The hotel joins the hotel switch at the top straight away, and Vento moves to it. Its settings show **Next steps**: **Set up rooms**, **Invite the team** (the invite is already limited to the new hotel) and **Register devices**. Each step ticks when it's done.

A new hotel is **Setting up** until its first night audit, then **Live**.

## Which hotel you see
The hotel switch at the top decides what Settings › Hotels shows. With **All hotels**, you see the list of every hotel; click one to open its settings, and **All hotels** above the title to go back. With one hotel chosen, you go straight to its settings.

## Change a hotel's details
Change any field and click **Save changes** (on a phone it's at the bottom of the screen). Everything applies at once, except:
- **Owning company** on a live hotel: Vento asks why, and the change starts on the next business date, so a day's takings are never split between two companies. Until then the field shows the new company and its start date, with **Cancel change**.
- **Time zone** is fixed once a hotel is live.

If someone else saved the same hotel while you were editing, Vento says who and when, and keeps your changes on screen. Click **Reload** to see theirs, then make yours again.

The business date shows under **Time and business date**. Only night audit moves it.

## Archive and restore a hotel
At the foot of a hotel's settings, click **Archive hotel**. Vento shows what happens first: how many rooms are kept, how many shared devices are signed out and revoked, and who works only at this hotel and will need another. Give a reason, which is recorded with your name, and click **Archive**. Nothing is deleted.

Vento won't archive your group's last hotel, or a hotel with something still open there.

Archived hotels are under **Archived** in the hotels list. Click **Restore**, give a reason, and the hotel comes back with its rooms and people's access. Its devices need registering again.

## Offline
Changing hotel details needs a connection. Offline, the page shows what was saved on your device, read-only, and the buttons say **Needs a connection**.
