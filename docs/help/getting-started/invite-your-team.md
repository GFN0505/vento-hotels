# Invite your team

Bring your managers and staff into Vento, choose what each one does, and decide which hotels they see. You need to be an owner or group admin (or have a role your owner has allowed to manage people).

## Invite people
1. Open **Settings** › **Members and roles**.
2. Click **Invite people**.
3. Type or paste their email addresses. Separate several with commas or new lines; you can invite up to 20 at a time.
4. Choose their **Role**, for example Front desk or General manager. What each role may do is set in **Permissions and limits**.
5. Under **Can see**, choose **Whole group** or **Only these hotels** and tick the hotels.
6. Click **Send invite**.

Each person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days. Their invite shows under **Invited** until they join.

Only an owner can invite another owner or a group admin. Owners and group admins always see every hotel.

## If someone didn't get the email
Under **Invited**, click **Resend**. They get a new link, and the old one stops working. Click **Cancel** to withdraw an invite.

## Change someone's role or hotels
Find them under **People** (search by name or email), click **Edit**, change their role or hotels, then **Save changes**. You can't change your own role; ask another owner.

## Remove someone
Click **Edit** › **Remove from group** and confirm. They're signed out of your group at once. Their name stays on everything they did, and you can invite them again later.

Every invite, change and removal is recorded in the audit log with your name.
