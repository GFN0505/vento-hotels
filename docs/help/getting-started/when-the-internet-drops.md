# When the internet or power drops

Vento keeps a copy of itself and of what you last saw on each device you use. When the connection drops:

- A **You're offline** banner appears under the top bar, saying when the data on screen was saved.
- You can still open Vento, even after a restart, and look at Home, your hotels and the menus.
- Changes (inviting someone, setting a PIN, changing a permission) need a connection for now. Try again when you're back online.
- When the connection returns, the banner goes by itself and the figures refresh.

Vento also notices when the Wi-Fi is on but the internet isn't working.

**On a new version:** when an update is ready, a message offers **Reload**. Finish what you're doing first; nothing changes until you tap it.

**Privacy:** signing out, or locking a shared device, removes everything Vento saved on it.

Taking orders, checking guests in and changing room status while offline arrive with the restaurant, front desk and housekeeping modules.
