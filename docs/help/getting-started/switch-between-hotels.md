# Switch between your hotels and the whole group

Home shows what matters for your job at the hotel you're working in. If you see every hotel in your group (owners, group admins and group finance), you can also see the whole group at once.

1. At the top left, click the hotel name (it says **Hotel** or **Group** before it).
2. Choose **All hotels** to see the group: key figures across the group, every hotel in a row, and what needs attention anywhere.
3. Click a hotel's row on Home, or choose it in the switch, to see that hotel's day.
4. Click **All hotels** in the banner to go back to the group.

Vento remembers your choice on this browser. On a shared device (a front desk PC or restaurant tablet) you always see that device's hotel.

## What your Home shows
- **Figures for your job.** Front desk sees arrivals, departures and in-house guests; housekeeping sees dirty rooms and inspections; waiters see their tables. Each figure fills in when its module goes live; until then the tile says which module brings it.
- **Your hotel:** its business date, check-in and check-out times, how many people work there and its shared devices.
- **Needs attention:** anything worth fixing now, with one button to fix it.
