# Switch between your hotels and the whole group

The hotel switch at the top left decides what every page shows. Pick a hotel and every page shows that hotel only; if you see every hotel in your group (owners, group admins and group finance), you can also pick **All hotels**.

1. At the top left, click the hotel name (it says **Hotel** or **Group** before it).
2. Choose **All hotels** to see the group: key figures across the group, every hotel in a row, and what needs attention anywhere.
3. Click a hotel's row on Home, or choose it in the switch, to see that hotel's day.
4. Click **All hotels** in the banner to go back to the group.

On a phone the switch takes the width of the top bar, and the business date is in your account menu (tap your initials). Vento remembers your choice on this browser. If you work at two or three hotels, the switch lists just those, without All hotels. If you work at one hotel there's no switch, and every page still names your hotel. On a shared device (a front desk PC or restaurant tablet) you always see that device's hotel.

## Every page follows the switch
- **Above each page's title** you'll see which hotel it shows ("Kora Abuja"), **All hotels**, or **Whole group** on pages that are the same everywhere, like Permissions and limits. The browser tab says it too.
- **Members and roles** with one hotel shows the people who work there, including people who see the whole group (marked **Whole group**), and the invites for that hotel. With All hotels it shows everyone.
- **Devices** with one hotel shows that hotel's shared devices; with All hotels, every device grouped by hotel, and which hotels have none yet (click one to register a device there).
- **Companies** with one hotel shows the company that owns it.
- **Rooms** is set up one hotel at a time, so with All hotels it asks you to pick one.
- When you change hotel, your search and filters stay, the list goes back to its first page, and anything you've typed but not saved asks before it's discarded.
- New invites and devices start on the hotel you're looking at. With All hotels, registering a device asks which hotel it's at.

## Links to a hotel
Links Vento gives you for one hotel (on Home, for example) open on that hotel. If a link is for a hotel you can't open, Vento says so and stays on the hotel you had.

## What your Home shows
- **Figures for your job.** Front desk sees arrivals, departures and in-house guests; housekeeping sees dirty rooms and inspections; waiters see their tables. Each figure fills in when its module goes live; until then the tile says which module brings it.
- **Your hotel:** its business date, check-in and check-out times, how many people work there and its shared devices.
- **Needs attention:** anything worth fixing now, with one button to fix it.
