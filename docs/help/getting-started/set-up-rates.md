# Set up your rates

Rate plans say what each room type costs and on what terms: what's included, the cancellation policy and the deposit. Set them up once at go-live (about 10 minutes for a hotel's usual plans), then change prices for seasons, weekends and events in the Rate calendar. You need to be the owner, the hotel's GM or a revenue manager to change rates. Front desk staff can read them, to quote a guest on the phone.

Every price in Vento is per room per night, in naira, and **includes taxes and service charge**: type the all-in price your guests already know. Vento works out the VAT, consumption tax and service charge inside it on every bill. With taxes set up, **Before taxes** under each price shows what's left once they're taken out.

If a plan's prices are before taxes, open it, go to **More options** and untick **Prices include taxes and service charge**. That plan is then marked **Excl. taxes** everywhere, and the taxes are added on top when it's booked. Plans you already have keep their setting.

## Your first rate
1. On **Home**, click **Set rates** in **Finish setting up**, or go to **Rates & channels** › **Rate plans**. If you see all your hotels, pick the one you're setting up. (Set up your rooms first: rates are set per room type.)
2. Type each room type's weekday price, for example 120,000 for Standard Queen. Leave **Fri and Sat** as "same", or type a different price for Friday and Saturday nights.
3. Leave **Also create a Bed & breakfast plan at +₦18,000** ticked if you sell breakfast. Bed & breakfast then always costs Best available plus ₦18,000, and the ₦18,000 counts as food revenue.
4. Click **Create Best available**.

Best available starts with the recommended terms: room only, free cancellation until 48 hours before arrival (then the first night), and a first-night deposit within 48 hours of booking. Open the plan to change any of them.

## Add a plan that follows another
Most hotels sell a few plans built on Best available, like Non-refundable at 10% less.

1. Click **New rate plan**.
2. Name it, for example Non-refundable. Vento suggests a code (NR); change it if you like (NRF).
3. Under **Price**, choose **Derived from a parent plan**, pick **Best available** and type an **Offset** in naira (18000, or −5000 for less) or a **percentage** (−10). The **Preview** shows tonight's price for each room type as you type, rounded to the whole naira.
4. Set the **Cancellation policy** and **Deposit rule**, for example Non-refundable with the full stay due at booking.
5. Click **Save plan** (or press ⌘S, Ctrl+S on Windows).

A derived plan follows its parent automatically: change Best available, or close a date on it, and Non-refundable changes too. You never type its prices.

Use **Duplicate** to start a new plan from an existing one. **Who can book it: Corporate accounts only** keeps a plan for companies with a contract; linking companies comes later.

## Change prices for dates
Go to **Rate calendar**. It shows 14 nights from today's business date (choose 30 nights if you like), with each room type's plans underneath. Friday and Saturday are tinted, and tonight's column says **Tonight**. A season sits above its dates with its range and min stay ("Festive season · 18 Dec–2 Jan · min 2"); click it to change those dates. On phones and tablets, **Key** explains the marks. Derived plans are greyed with a link icon: change their parent instead.

Under each room type's name, **Rooms left** shows how many rooms of that type are still free on each night: the same number as the tape chart's Available row and New reservation. A room out of order and every booked night count against it. 0 (sold out) is red and 1 or 2 are amber; a night with more bookings than rooms shows −1 in red. Hover over a red or amber number to read it in words ("Sold out, 1 out of order"). Front desk staff see it too, so "Any Deluxe Kings on Friday?" is one look. You can't change rooms left here: take rooms off sale with out of order on the tape chart.

- **One night:** click the price, type the new one and press Enter. A toast confirms it with **Undo**.
- **A range:** click **Change dates**. Choose the plan, room types, dates and days of the week, then **New prices** (one per room type) or **Back to standard price**. Add a **Season name** such as Festive season if you like, and set **Min stay**, **Closed to arrival** or **Closed** (each stays as it is unless you choose On or Off). The sheet says how many dates change and which plans follow. Click **Apply**, and **Undo** in the toast if it wasn't right.

With the keyboard, Tab into the calendar and use the arrow keys to move between nights and plans; press Enter or start typing to change a price. Click any price to see it in words, ready to read to a caller: "Deluxe King · Bed & breakfast · Sat 5 Dec · ₦183,000 · min stay 2".

On a phone, the calendar shows one room type at a time, night by night, with its rooms left in each night's heading ("4 left"). Pick the room type at the top; **Change dates** sits beside it. Tap a price to change it.

Dates before the business date can't change. Bookings already made keep the price they were sold at.

## Lowering tonight's price
Once your hotel is live, lowering tonight's price (on any plan, or by changing a parent plan) asks for a reason. It's recorded with your name and shows in the owner's daily report. Raising a price, or changing future dates, never asks.

## Archive a plan
Open the plan and click **Archive plan**. It stops being offered for new bookings; bookings already made keep their price and terms. Find it under **Archived** on the Rate plans page, with **Restore**. A plan that others follow can't be archived until they're archived or follow another plan. A plan never used on a booking can be deleted for good from Archived.

## Offline
Changing rates needs a connection, so every device gets the same prices. Offline, Rate plans and the Rate calendar show what was saved on your device, read-only, and the buttons say **Needs a connection**. Rooms left are as they were when the calendar was last updated, so check again once you're back online before promising the last room.
