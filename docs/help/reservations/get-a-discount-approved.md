# Get a discount approved by a manager

When a guest asks for more off than your limit allows, a manager with a higher limit can approve it, at your desk with their PIN or from their own phone. You book it yourself once they say yes. Both names, the reason and the device are kept for the owner.

Your limit is set per role in **Permissions and limits** (front desk 10% by default, revenue managers 25%, GMs 50%, the owner any). A manager can approve up to their own limit, only at a hotel they work at, and never their own discount.

## Ask at the desk (the manager is nearby)
1. In **New reservation**, on **Room & rate**, click **Change rate** and type the discount with a reason. Over your limit, Vento says "15% is over your 12% discount limit. A manager with a higher limit can approve it when you book." and you can carry on.
2. On **Review & book**, click **Ask for approval · 15%**.
3. The prompt shows the plan price, the new rate, the stay total before and after, and your reason. Tap the manager's name.
4. The manager types their 4-digit PIN and clicks **Approve 15% and book**. The booking is made at once; its page says "Approved by Tunde Bello". You stay signed in.

A manager can type their PIN only on a shared hotel device registered to that hotel (like the front desk PC). Wrong PINs count with the lock screen's: after five, their PIN is locked for 15 minutes. After four wrong PINs typed at your screen, Vento pauses approvals here for 15 minutes; send it to an inbox instead.

## Send it to the manager's inbox (the manager isn't nearby)
1. In the prompt, tap the manager's name, then **Not nearby? Send to Tunde's inbox** (on your own laptop, this is the only choice).
2. **Review & book** shows "Waiting for Tunde Bello · asked 10:36 · expires 11:06". You can **Cancel request**. The room isn't held while you wait.
3. The manager sees the bell in the top bar light up, taps **Review**, checks the figures and approves with their PIN, or declines with a comment.
4. Within seconds your screen says "Approved by Tunde Bello at 10:39" and the button reads **Book**. Click it.

If your screen locked while you waited, sign back in and open the bell: **Your requests** has **Continue booking**, which reopens the booking at Review.

## If the answer is no, or doesn't come
- **Declined:** you see the manager's comment. Click **Change discount** to go back to Room & rate.
- **No answer in 30 minutes:** the request expires. Click **Ask again** or change the discount.
- **An approval lasts 30 minutes** after it's given, and only for that business date. Changing the dates, room, plan, guests or discount cancels a waiting request; ask again for the new price.
- You can have up to 5 requests waiting at once.

## For managers: answer requests
Open the bell, or **See all** for the **Approvals** page: **Waiting** (asked of you), **Asked by you** and **Done**. With All hotels you see every hotel you work at; pick a hotel to see just its requests. Decline needs a comment so the person who asked knows why.

## Offline
Approvals need a connection. Offline, **Ask for approval**, **Approve** and **Decline** are disabled, and the Approvals page shows what was loaded before you went offline.
