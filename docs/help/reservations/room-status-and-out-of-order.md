# Mark a room clean, or put it out of order

## Mark a room clean or dirty
When housekeeping says a room is done, click the room's number on the tape chart (or tap it on a phone) and choose **Mark clean**. Choose **Mark dirty** when it needs cleaning. The toast has **Undo**. Vento records who did it and when ("Clean · Grace Akpan · 11:10").

Front desk, housekeeping and managers can do this. It works without a connection: the room shows "Will sync" and the change is sent when you're back. If someone else changed the same room later, their change is kept and you're told ("502's status changed to dirty by Grace Akpan at 11:20 after your change at 11:05. Kept as dirty.").

## Put a room out of order
GMs, housekeeping supervisors, maintenance and the owner can take a room off sale.
1. Click the room's number, then **Put out of order**.
2. Choose **From** (today or later) and **Back in service** (the first night it can be sold again).
3. Pick a reason (AC, Plumbing, Electrical, Renovation, Other) and add a note if it helps.
4. Vento checks as you go. If a booking is in the room on those nights, it says so with a button to move it ("Room 503 has Emeka Kalu on 25–27 Nov. Move the booking first."). If taking the room off would leave too few rooms for the bookings of that type, it says which night ("Executive King would be 1 room short on 26 Nov."), and nothing is saved.
5. Click **Put out of order · 2 nights**.

Rooms left for that type drop on those nights everywhere, New reservation included. A room put out of order from today is set dirty. On a live hotel, every room taken off sale, and every extension, is in the owner's daily report with your name.

## Change dates, back in service, or remove
Click the room's number again: **Change dates** (only the end, once it has started), **Back in service** (it ends today; choose whether the room is dirty or clean), or **Remove** for one that hasn't started. Each asks why.

Out of order needs a connection.
