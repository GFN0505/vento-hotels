# Use the tape chart

The tape chart shows every room of a hotel by night: who is in which room, which rooms are free, which are out of order, and how many rooms of each type are left. Open **Tape chart** in the menu and pick the hotel in the top bar (with All hotels chosen, it asks which).

## Read it
- **Tonight** at the top says how many rooms are occupied (a guest checked in), arriving (a booking in the room, not in yet), vacant and out of order tonight, and how many bookings for tonight have no room yet; the Front desk shows the same figures. Click a figure to see just those rooms (click again to see all); "not in a room yet" opens the Unassigned rows.
- Each room says who is in it tonight first: **Occupied** (checked in), **Arriving** (arrives today), **Leaving** (leaves today), **Vacant** or **Out of order**, with its cleaning status under it as a dot (Clean, Inspected, Dirty). Hover it for both together, like "Vacant · Dirty".
- A guest in house is a solid blue bar; a booking over tonight not checked in yet is a solid violet bar; one starting later is a lighter bar; one that has ended is grey. A guest who moved rooms shows their earlier nights greyed out in the old room, with a mark where they moved. An arrow at the start means the guest arrives today. Tonight's column is outlined down the chart, and an empty room tonight shows **Free** in it.
- Rooms are rows, grouped by room type and floor; nights are columns, starting two nights before the business date (highlighted). Choose **7**, **14** or **30 days**, step a week with the arrows, and press **T** (or **Today**) to come back.
- Each booking is a bar from arrival to departure with the guest's name. A hatched rose block is a room out of order.
- **Available** under each room type is how many rooms are left that night (rose at 0). **Unassigned** at the top of a type shows bookings with no room yet; click it to see them as bars.
- Filter by room type, floor, or **Unassigned** to see only types with bookings waiting for a room.

## Give a booking a room, or move it
- Drag a bar onto a room. Rooms free for every night of the stay light up; a taken room says why ("402 · Shittu R. 26–27 Nov"). The toast has **Undo**.
- Or click the bar, then **Room move** (or **Assign room**), pick a room (ready rooms come first when the guest arrives today) and click **Move to 401**. A reason is optional.
- You see only rooms of the room type the guest booked. Other room types appear, as upgrades, only when no room of the booked type is free for every night of the stay; dropping a bar on another type's room says so while one is.
- Drag a bar back onto its type's **Unassigned** row, or use **Remove room** in the dialog, to take the room off.
- Moving to another room type (when the booked type is full) changes the price, so Vento shows it first: **Charge Executive King rate**, or **Keep ₦168,000.00 a night** (an upgrade at the booked price, recorded as a discount with reason Upgrade). Over your discount limit, **Ask for approval** lets a manager approve it with their PIN, as for any discount.

## Move a guest who is in house
Drag the bar onto another room, or use **Room move**: the move starts tonight (the nights already slept stay in the old room), a reason is always asked for, and the old room is set dirty. Click a bar for **Check in** on today's arrivals, or **Print registration card** for a guest in house.

## Extend or shorten a stay
Drag the right end of a bar to the new departure. The box shows the nights added (at today's rate) or taken off, with service charge and taxes, and whether the room is free. Click **Extend stay** (or **Shorten stay**). If the room is taken on a new night, move the stay first or use **Modify** (press E on a bar) and leave it unassigned.

## Book from the chart
Click an empty night of a room (today or later): New reservation opens on that room type and night, and the room is assigned when you book. A night that can't be sold (out of order, booked, or the room type is full) doesn't open a booking; hover it to see why. If you change the dates to cover a night the room isn't free, Vento says so before you book; pick other dates or **Book without a room**.

## Keyboard
Click into the chart, then use the arrow keys to move between rooms and nights. **Enter** opens what's there (a booking's card, the room's menu on its number, or New reservation on a free night). On a booking, **M** opens Room move and **E** opens Modify. **Escape** cancels a drag.

## On a phone
The chart becomes a list of one night: step the date with the arrows, tap a room for its actions (Mark clean or dirty, Room move, Modify), or tap **Unassigned** to give an arrival a room.

## Offline
The chart shows what it last loaded, with the time. Marking rooms clean or dirty still works and syncs when you're back. Moving rooms, extending, booking and out of order need a connection.
