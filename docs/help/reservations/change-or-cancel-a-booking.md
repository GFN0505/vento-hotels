# Change or cancel a booking

When a guest calls to add a night, change their dates or room type, or cancel, open the reservation and use **Modify** or **Cancel**. Vento shows the new price, which rooms are free and what the cancellation policy costs before you save. Everything you change is kept with your name and device.

## Change dates, room type or guests
1. Find the reservation (search by name, phone or number) and open it.
2. Click **Modify** (or press **M**).
3. Change **Arrive**, **Leave**, **Room type** or the number of guests. Within a moment the box shows each new night "at today's rate", the taxes, and the stay total before and after (₦504,000.00 → ₦672,000.00), and whether the room type is free on the new nights.
4. Click **Save · +₦168,000.00**. The reservation and its Activity show the change.

What stays and what changes:
- **Nights the guest already agreed keep their price**, even if the rate has gone up since. New nights, and every night when you change the room type, are priced from the same rate plan at today's price.
- **The rate plan can't change.** To move a guest to another plan, cancel and book again; the cancellation follows the policy.
- **Room types the plan doesn't sell aren't listed.** A type that is full on a new night shows "Sold out 12 Dec" and can't be picked; one too small for the party shows "Sleeps 2 adults".
- **The deposit due and free cancellation** are worked out again from the booking's terms. If free cancellation has already ended, moving the dates doesn't start it again.

### A discount on the new nights
If the booking has a discount, new nights get the same %. That counts as a discount you give: within your limit it saves; over it, the button reads **Ask for approval · 15%** and a manager approves it with their PIN at your desk or from their inbox ([Get a discount approved by a manager](get-a-discount-approved.md)). Or tick **Price new nights without the discount**. **Change rate** sets a new discount on every night or removes it.

### Shortening after free cancellation has ended
If the change lowers the stay total after the deadline (or on a non-refundable plan), Vento says so before you save: "Free changes ended 7 Dec 2026, 14:00. This change lowers the stay by ₦165,375.00, so a penalty of ₦165,375.00 applies." You can waive it like a cancellation penalty (below).

## Cancel a booking
1. Open the reservation and click **Cancel**.
2. The box shows the policy, the penalty if any, and the nights released. Free before the deadline: "Free cancellation until 7 Dec 2026, 14:00 · No penalty". A booking with no deposit or guarantee is released without a penalty.
3. Pick a **Reason** (Guest request, Company request, Deposit not paid, Duplicate booking, or Other with a few words) and add a note if it helps.
4. Click **Cancel booking** (or **Cancel booking · ₦257,250.00 penalty**). The rooms are free for others at once, the reservation shows **Cancelled** with who, when and why, and the **Penalty** card shows "To charge".

Click **Copy cancellation** to paste a message for the guest into WhatsApp. There is no un-cancel: book again if the guest changes their mind.

## Waive the penalty
Tick **Waive the penalty** and say why. It waives the whole amount.
- **Revenue managers, GMs and the owner** waive it themselves: the button reads **Cancel and waive ₦257,250.00**.
- **Front desk:** the button reads **Ask to waive ₦257,250.00**. Pick the manager. If they're at the desk, they type their PIN and click **Approve waiver and cancel**: the booking is cancelled and the penalty waived together. If not, tap **Not nearby? Cancel now and send the waiver to Tunde's inbox**: the booking is cancelled at once and the Penalty card says "Waiver asked of Tunde Bello" until he answers. When he approves, it turns to "Waived · Approved by Tunde Bello" on its own.

A penalty still to charge can be waived later from the **Penalty** card (**Waive penalty**, or **Ask to waive** / **Ask again**). Every waiver is in the owner's daily report with both names, the amount and the reason.

## Which bookings can be changed
- Confirmed bookings arriving today or later can be modified and cancelled.
- Once the arrival date has passed, Modify and Cancel are gone: "Arrival was 24 Nov 2026. Mark it as a no-show." ([Mark a no-show](mark-a-no-show.md)).
- Cancelled and no-show bookings can't be changed.

## If someone else changed it first
If a colleague changed or cancelled the booking while you had it open, Vento says so ("Sade Bakare changed this booking at 11:08. Check the new details.") and shows the new details; nothing of yours is saved. If the price moved meanwhile, you see the new figures to check with the guest.

## Offline
Changing or cancelling needs a connection, because availability, the penalty and approvals are only true on the server. Offline, the buttons are disabled with "Changing a booking needs a connection." The reservation stays readable.
