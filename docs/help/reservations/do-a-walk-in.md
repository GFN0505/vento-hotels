# Do a walk-in

For a guest without a booking. Press **W** on the Front desk (or **Walk-in**). The page starts with tonight, one night, one adult, and a timer.

1. **Change dates** sets the departure (up to 30 nights) and the party.
2. **Room type:** each type shows its price tonight and how many rooms are ready ("2 ready · 302 first"). Types too small for the party, or sold out on a night, can't be picked. Picking a type picks its first ready room; **Change** to pick another.
3. **Rate:** the hotel's Best available is chosen. **Change rate** gives a discount with a reason; over your limit, **Ask for approval** lets a manager approve it with their PIN.
4. **Guest:** type the phone number first; a returning guest shows up to pick. Then the name, nationality, ID type and number, home address, and the **ID photo** (Take photo or Upload scan).
5. **Check in to 302** (⌘↵). The booking is made and the guest checked in at once; the card prints if ticked. **Undo** within 8 seconds cancels the booking too.

Walk-ins need a connection (Vento checks live availability). Payment comes with the folio.
