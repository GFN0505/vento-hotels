# Check in a guest

Open **Front desk** in the menu (it opens on **Arrivals**). Every guest arriving today is listed with their room and whether it is ready: **Inspected** or **Clean** (ready), **Dirty**, **Due out** (the room's guest leaves today and hasn't gone yet) or **Not in a room**.

## Check in
1. Click **Check in** on the guest's row (or move to it with **J** and **K** and press **C**).
2. **Verify ID.** Check the guest's ID matches them, then **Take photo** (the laptop's camera or a phone's) or **Upload scan**. You see the photo before anything is saved; **Retake** if it isn't sharp. A returning guest's photo on file can be kept.
3. **Registration.** What the law asks for is marked; the rest is under **More details**. For a guest who isn't Nigerian, the **Foreign national registration** opens by itself: passport, expiry, occupation, visa or permit (**Not required · ECOWAS citizen** for ECOWAS nationals), arriving from and next destination. A returning guest's details are filled in ("Same as last stay · ••••••6612"); **Change** to type a new number.
4. **Room.** The assigned room is shown with who cleaned it and when. **Change room** lists the free rooms of the booked type, ready ones first. A dirty room can be chosen: the guest registers now and gets the key when it's clean, and the stay shows **Waiting for room** until it is.
5. **Check in to 502.** The registration card prints if **Print registration card** is ticked (it stays ticked on this computer). The toast has **Undo** for 8 seconds.

A returning guest with a room ready usually takes two clicks: **Check in**, then **Check in to 404**.

## When something is missing
- Vento moves you to the first thing missing, with what to type ("Enter the next destination").
- No camera? A GM or the owner can **Skip for now** with a reason; the stay then shows **ID photo to add**, and **Add ID photo** on the reservation adds it later.
- Passport or visa ending before departure: Vento warns you, but you can still check the guest in.

## After check-in
- The guest is on **In house**, with the night of their stay ("Night 2 of 3").
- **Print registration card** on the reservation prints it again (a reprint says "REPRINT · copy 2").
- A check-in made by mistake, after Undo has gone: a GM opens the reservation, **More → Reverse check-in**, and gives a reason. It's in the owner's report the next day. Nobody reverses their own check-in.
- ID and passport numbers are shown masked. A GM or the owner can **Reveal** one for 30 seconds, or **View ID photo**, with a reason; it's recorded with their name.

## Offline
An arrival already in a room can be checked in with the internet down: type every required field, take the photo, and **Check in to 502**. It shows **Will sync** and goes through when you're back. Picking another room, walk-ins, Reveal and printing need a connection. The lists stay on the front desk computer when it locks, so the next person can carry on.
