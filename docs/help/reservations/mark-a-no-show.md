# Mark a no-show

When a guest with a booking for tonight hasn't arrived and won't, mark the booking as a no-show. The rooms are released at once, so a late walk-in can take them, and the no-show penalty under the booking's terms is recorded.

Mark a no-show only when the guest won't arrive tonight. A guest who turns up after being marked is booked again, and the penalty can be waived with a reason.

## Mark it
1. Open the reservation (arrival today, or an earlier date the night audit hasn't closed).
2. Click **⋯ More**, then **Mark no-show**.
3. The box shows the policy ("Best available: a no-show is charged the first night"), the penalty and the nights released.
4. Add a note if it helps ("Didn't call, phone off") and click **Mark no-show · ₦147,000.00 penalty**.

After midnight, before night audit, the business date hasn't moved: last night's arrivals are still that day's and can be marked.

## Waive the penalty
If the guest had a good reason (a cancelled flight), tick **Waive the penalty** and say why. Managers waive it themselves (**Mark no-show and waive ₦183,750.00**). At the front desk, **Ask to waive** sends it to a manager: their PIN at your desk does both at once; or **Not nearby? Mark no-show now and send the waiver to Tunde's inbox** marks the no-show now and he approves from his phone. The Penalty card turns to "Waived · Approved by Tunde Bello" when he does.

If the manager declines or doesn't answer in 30 minutes, the penalty stays to charge and you can **Ask again** from the Penalty card.
