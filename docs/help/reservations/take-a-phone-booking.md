# Take a phone booking

When a guest calls, sends a WhatsApp or emails for a room, book it in **New reservation**: about a minute from "Do you have a Deluxe for 9 to 12 December?" to a confirmation you can paste back to them. Front desk, revenue managers, GMs and the owner can make bookings, on their own sign-in or with their PIN on the front desk PC. Finance and the night auditor can read bookings but not make them.

## Book a room
1. Go to **Reservations** and click **New reservation** (or press **N**). If you see all your hotels, pick the **Hotel** first.
2. **Dates & guests:** pick the arrival and departure dates and the number of adults and children. Arrival starts on today's business date. Press **Enter** or click **Continue**.
3. **Room & rate:** each room type shows how many rooms are left on each night of the stay: amber at 3 or fewer, red at 0. A type that's sold out on any night says so ("Sold out 11 Dec") and can't be picked. Click a plan to choose it. Prices per night show **Incl. taxes** or **Excl. taxes** (incl. by default; your choice is remembered on this computer); the total for the stay always includes taxes. The summary on the right shows every night, the service charge and taxes, the total, the deposit due and the cancellation terms. Counts refresh every 30 seconds while you're on this step.
4. **Guest:** type 3 or more letters of the guest's name, phone or email in **Find a guest**. Pick them if they've stayed with the group before: their phone and email show partly hidden (+234 803 ••• 4567), so confirm them with the guest and type a new one only if it has changed. Or fill in a new guest: title, first and last name, a phone number or an email, and nationality. A Nigerian number can start with 0; foreign numbers need their country code (+44 7700 900123). Choose **How they booked** (Phone, WhatsApp, Email or In person) and add any **Notes**, like "Late arrival around 22:00". For a foreign guest the summary also shows the total in their currency at today's CBN rate, for reference only: bills are always in naira.
If **Continue** can't go on yet, it says what's missing.
5. **Review & book:** check everything with the guest, then click **Book** (or press ⌘↵, Ctrl+Enter on Windows).

The booking opens with its number, for example VI-2611-0483. Click **Copy confirmation** and paste it into WhatsApp or an email: it has the stay, the total, the deposit with the hotel's bank account and the reference to use, the cancellation terms and the check-in time and address. Vento doesn't send messages itself yet.

## Give a discount
On **Room & rate**, click **Change rate** under the summary. Type a **Discount** in % or a **Rate per night** in naira (each fills in the other), pick a **Reason** and add a note if you like. The discount is on every night of the stay and is recorded with your name; the owner sees it in the next morning's report.

Your role has a limit (front desk 10% by default, revenue managers 25%, GMs 50%; the owner sets them in **Permissions and limits**). Above it, Vento says so and a manager with a higher limit can approve it when you book: see [Get a discount approved by a manager](get-a-discount-approved.md). A rate can't be above the plan's price, ₦0 (a free night is a comp) or below the breakfast part of a breakfast plan.

## Booking through a restriction
If a plan's dates have a min stay, are closed, or are closed to arrival, the plan says so in amber and you can still pick it. **Review & book** then asks for a reason, which is recorded with your name.

## If something changes while you book
- **Sold while you were booking:** if someone else books the last room first, you'll see "Deluxe King was just sold for 11 Dec" with what's still free. Nothing is saved; pick again.
- **Price changed:** if a rate changed after you quoted it, nothing is saved and you'll see the new price. Check it with the guest and book again.
- **Closed the window by mistake:** open **New reservation** again; your draft is still there until you book, discard it or sign out. Drafts are never saved on the computer.

## Find a booking again
In **Reservations**, press **/** and type the guest's name, part of their phone number or email, or the last digits of the booking number. **Upcoming** shows bookings still to come, **Booked today** everything taken on today's business date, and **All** everything. Filter by status, room type, how they booked, arrival dates or deposits due.

## Offline
New bookings need a connection, because rooms left are only certain on the server. Offline, **New reservation** and **Book** are disabled, and the list shows what was last loaded. If the connection dropped just as you clicked **Book**, keep the window open: Vento checks whether it went through when you're back online, and never books twice.
