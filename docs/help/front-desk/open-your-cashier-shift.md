# Open your cashier shift

A cashier shift is your turn on one cash drawer, from the float you accept at the start to the count you make at the end. Open one before you take any money. Open **Cash** in the menu; the hotel is the one picked in the top bar (with All hotels chosen, it asks which).

## Open it
1. Pick the drawer you're working at, for example **Desk 1**. The drawer you used last on this computer is picked for you. A drawer someone else is using shows their name ("Desk 1 · Chiamaka Eze's shift") and can't be picked. If every drawer is busy, ask finance to add one, or wait for one to close.
2. Count the float you were handed, by note: how many ₦1,000, ₦500, ₦200, ₦100 and ₦50 notes, and the total of smaller notes and coins. Each row shows its total, and **Float counted** adds them up as you type. Press Tab to go down the rows.
3. Check the **Hand-over** on the right. It shows who left the float, from which shift and when, how much they left, and how your count compares: "matches", "₦2,000.00 short" or "₦2,000.00 over". On a drawer's first shift there's nothing to compare: count what you were given.
4. Press **Open shift · ₦50,000.00 float** (or Enter). If you were given no float, the button says **Open shift with no float**.

Your shift is now open: "Cashier shift 1", with your name, the drawer, the time and the float. Takings aren't shown while it's open, so your count at the end is your own.

## If your count doesn't match the hand-over
Add a **Reason for the difference**, for example "Topped up the float from the safe". It's recorded with your name and shows in the owner's daily report.

Within your limit (₦5,000.00 for front desk and cashiers), the reason is enough. Above it, the button becomes **Ask for approval**. Pick a manager: on the hotel's registered front desk PC they type their PIN on your screen and the shift opens at once (**Approve and open shift**). Otherwise send the request to their inbox; your screen shows "Waiting for Tunde Bello" until they approve from their phone, then you open the shift. You can't approve your own difference. If you change the count, the drawer or the reason after asking, the request is cancelled and you ask again.

## Good to know
- You can have one shift open at a time, at any hotel of the group, and a drawer has one shift open at a time. If your shift is open at another hotel, Cash says where, with a button to go there.
- Opening a shift needs a connection.
- Shift numbers start from 1 each business date at each hotel.
