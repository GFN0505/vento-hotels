# Count and close your cashier shift

At the end of your shift you count everything you're holding without seeing what Vento expects (a blind count), submit it once, and then close the shift, leaving a float for the next person and taking the rest to the safe. Open **Cash** in the menu, where your open shift is shown.

## Count
1. Press **Count and close**.
2. Count the naira in the drawer, including the float, by note, and the smaller notes and coins.
3. Under **Other methods**, enter:
   - foreign cash, one amount per currency the hotel takes ("Cash US$", "Cash £");
   - for each POS terminal at your drawer, the total of your merchant slips this shift (enter 0 if it took nothing);
   - the total of bank transfers you confirmed this shift (enter 0 if none).
   POS and transfers must be filled in, even with 0; if one is empty, Vento takes you to it.
4. Press **Submit count**. Vento shows every figure once more ("Cash ₦50,000.00 · US$0.00 · £0.00 · POS ₦0.00 · Transfers ₦0.00"). Check them and press **Submit count** again, or **Keep counting**.

Once submitted, the count can't be changed by anyone. If you leave the page before submitting, what you typed is lost and you count again.

## Close
After you submit, you see for the first time what was expected on each line, what you counted and any difference ("−₦2,500.00 short", "+₦12,500.00 over"). The **Close shift** panel says **Balanced**, **Short** or **Over**.

1. If there's a difference, add a **Reason for the difference**, for example "Change given in error". It's recorded with your name and every difference shows in the owner's daily report.
2. **Float stays in drawer** is the naira you leave for the next person (your opening float to start with). It can't be more than the naira you counted.
3. **To the safe** is the rest of the naira, plus all foreign cash. If anything goes to the safe, enter the **Safe bag number**, for example S-2211.
4. Press **Close shift** (or **Close shift · drop ₦409,500.00 to safe**).

Above your limit (₦5,000.00 for front desk and cashiers), press **Ask for approval** and pick a manager: they type their PIN on the hotel's front desk PC (**Approve and close shift**), or approve from their inbox while your screen waits. If they decline, the shift stays counted, not closed; you can ask someone else, or again with another reason. If nobody can approve it, ask finance or the owner.

The drawer is then free, holding the float you left, and Cash offers **Open a new shift**.

## Your shifts
**Cash › Shifts** lists your shifts, newest first, with the float, any difference and who approved it. Click one to see its counts, reasons and history. GMs, finance and owners see every shift of the hotel.

## Offline
You can keep typing your count while offline, and it stays on the screen; submitting, closing and approvals need a connection. If your connection dropped just as you submitted, Vento checks when it's back and never submits twice.
