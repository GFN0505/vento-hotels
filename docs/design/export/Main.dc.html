Typeface: Inter, with tabular figures

Proposal replaces Poppins in the product

Poppins is a geometric display face: round, wide letters and a short x-height. In a 14-column tape chart or a folio with VAT, consumption tax and service charge lines, that width costs a column on a 1440 screen and blurs at 12–13px. Its figures are proportional by default, so ₦1,111.00 and ₦8,888.00 don't align.

Inter was drawn for screens at 11–16px: tall x-height, open apertures, a distinct 1 l I, and true tabular figures (tnum) so money, room numbers and counts sit in straight columns. Keep Poppins for the guest-facing booking engine headings if the hotel brand wants it.

UX decisions worth explaining

Business date is a chip, not a clock

After midnight the top bar turns the chip amber and shows both dates ("Business date 24 Nov · Calendar 25 Nov"). Postings always use the business date until night audit rolls it, so the agent sees why.

Taxes are never hidden in a total

Every price shows its basis ("excl. taxes") and every total breaks out service charge 10%, VAT 7.5% and state consumption tax 5% as their own lines. Rates in the demo are exclusive; inclusive properties show "incl." and back-calculate the same lines.

Offline is a state of each record

The top bar dot is quiet when online. Offline, a banner appears and each action made on the device carries a "Saved on this device" tag until it syncs, so nobody re-enters a payment.

Reasons live in the same dialog

Overrides, voids, comps and refunds ask for a reason inline. Above the user's limit the same dialog grows a manager PIN field instead of failing, so the guest isn't kept waiting.

Leakage is reported, not alleged

Exception views state facts (who, device, reason, approver) in neutral words. No red "suspicious" labels: rose is reserved for things that are actually overdue, over limit or unresolved.

Keyboard first at the desk

⌘K reaches any guest, room or folio and runs actions ("Check in 214"). Every primary button shows its shortcut; flows advance with Enter, steps jump with ⌘1–5.
